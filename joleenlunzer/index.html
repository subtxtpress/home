Who is this pale girl anyway?

Joleen Lunzer is a Los Angeles–based stand-up comedian and writer. Her comedy has been featured on FOX, Hulu, and BET. She recently filmed for the upcoming 3rd season of Laugh After Dark on Amazon, and co-produces The Mermaid Comedy Hour, a monthly showcase at the Hollywood Improv.

Joleen has also performed for the troops with Armed Forces Entertainment, traveling to Kosovo, Germany, Belgium, Austria, Italy, and Spain to bring the laughs overseas. On top of all that, she's a lifelong Buffy the Vampire Slayer enthusiast — so yes, she will absolutely out-quote you.
