About

Trained for Structures That Require a Different Approach

Historical and heritage-grade buildings operate under different constraints than modern construction. Our methods, material selection, and safety planning reflect those constraints.

Who We Serve

Clients with Preservation and Heritage-Grade Requirements

We support clients who maintain or restore historical and heritage-grade structures. This includes public agencies, preservation architects, heritage organizations, institutional property managers, and homeowners with long-standing or multi-generational properties.

We also work in post-disaster environments where climate events have affected structures with preservation value — properties where standard repair methods would compromise original materials or architectural elements.

Our client base does not include standard residential construction or general commercial projects. Each project is reviewed before acceptance to confirm it meets our criteria.

Why We Specialize

Historical Structures Require Different Risk Controls

Historical and heritage-grade buildings present conditions that are not addressed by standard roofing or scaffolding practices. Load-bearing assumptions, material compatibility, surface fragility, and access constraints differ significantly from modern construction.

Scaffolding systems that are appropriate for new builds can cause surface damage, structural stress, or compliance violations when applied to aged masonry, slate, clay tile, or ornamental elements. Roofing materials must be matched to the building's period, climate history, and any applicable preservation guidelines.

Our team is trained to work within these constraints, including sites listed on local, state, or national registers, and properties managed under active preservation plans or heritage easements.

Our Approach

Controlled Methods, Documented Outcomes

We apply a structured approach to every project to protect both the structure and the preservation record.

  • Controlled installation methods. All scaffolding and roofing work uses procedures specific to the surface type, structural age, and site access conditions. Non-invasive anchoring systems are used wherever required.

  • Documentation for preservation records. We provide written documentation of materials used, installation methods, and any observed conditions that are relevant to the preservation record or insurance requirements.

  • Material selection aligned with historical requirements. Where preservation standards specify period-appropriate materials, we source and install to that specification. Substitutions are documented and require client approval.

  • Safety plans designed for fragile or restricted sites. Each site receives an individual safety plan that accounts for surface vulnerability, access limitations, and any regulatory restrictions associated with the property's preservation status.

Our Commitment

We protect the integrity of the structure and complete all work without altering or damaging original architectural elements. Where damage is pre-existing or where conditions require deviation from the preservation plan, we stop work, document the condition, and consult with the client and any relevant preservation authority before proceeding.
