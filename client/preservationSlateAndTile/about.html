About

Slate and Tile Specialists for Structures That Cannot Afford a Mistake

Natural slate and clay tile roofing on historical buildings requires training, tooling, and material knowledge that general roofing contractors do not have. We specialize in these systems because the work demands it.

Who We Serve

Preservation Architects, Heritage Contractors, and Historic Property Owners

We work with preservation architects, heritage-grade contractors, institutional property managers, and homeowners maintaining historical or multi-generational structures with existing slate, clay tile, or copper roofing systems.

We also work with owners of post-disaster properties where the existing roof system has preservation value and standard replacement methods would compromise the original material, tile profile, or architectural record.

Our project intake is limited to structures where the roofing material itself — slate, clay tile, or copper — is a load-bearing part of the preservation scope. General residential and commercial projects are not accepted.

Why We Specialize

Natural Slate and Clay Tile Are Not Standard Roofing Materials

Slate and clay tile are dimensionally variable, load-sensitive, and installation-dependent in ways that asphalt and synthetic systems are not. Mismatched replacement slate, incorrect fastening gauge, or improper flashing integration can cause failure in materials that would otherwise last another century.

Copper flashing on a heritage roof is fabricated to specific profiles and thicknesses. Standard flashing products are not compatible. Valley geometry, chimney saddle design, and gutter expansion behavior differ from modern systems and must be matched to the original installation.

We are trained through Ludowici Factory Install Training, TRI Alliance, the National Slate Association, and the Slate Roofing Contractors Association specifically in the materials and systems we install. That training is not transferable from general roofing work.

Our Approach

Period-Matched Materials, Documented Installation

We apply a structured approach to every project to protect both the structure and the preservation record.

  • Slate and tile matched to specification. Replacement slates are sourced by quarry region, thickness, and color blend to match the existing field. Clay tile is matched by profile — S-curve, flat, English, or French — and by manufacturer where records exist.

  • Copper fabricated on-site. Valleys, flashings, chimney saddles, gutters, and ridgework are fabricated at the project location to the dimensions and profile of the existing metalwork. No off-the-shelf flashing products are used on heritage roofs.

  • Documentation for preservation and insurance records. All work is documented: materials used, source, installation method, and pre-existing conditions observed during scope. Documentation is provided in a format compatible with state and local preservation office requirements.

  • Individual safety plans for fragile surfaces. Slate and clay tile surfaces require different load distribution and access controls than modern roofing. Each site receives a safety plan designed for the specific surface type, structural age, and any preservation restrictions on the property.

Our Commitment

We do not substitute materials without client and preservation authority approval. Where conditions on the roof differ from what was documented in scope — including undiscovered damage, incompatible underlying systems, or non-original prior repairs — we stop work, document the condition, and consult with the client and any applicable preservation authority before proceeding.

Provide the roof material, building age, preservation status, and a summary of required work. We will confirm whether the project meets our acceptance criteria and schedule accordingly.
