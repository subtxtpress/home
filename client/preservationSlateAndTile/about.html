About

Specialist in Historic Slate, Clay Tile, and Period Copper Roof Systems

Historic slate, clay tile, and period copper each have material sourcing, installation, and safety requirements specific to their system. Projects are reviewed before scheduling to confirm they meet preservation and heritage-grade criteria.

Who We Serve

Preservation Architects, Heritage Contractors, and Historic Property Owners

We work with preservation architects, heritage contractors, institutional property managers, and homeowners maintaining historic or multi-generational structures with existing slate, clay tile, or copper roofing systems.

We also work with owners of post-disaster properties where the existing roof system has preservation significance and standard replacement methods would compromise the original material, tile profile, or architectural record.

We accept projects only where slate, clay tile, or copper roofing is part of the preservation scope. General residential and commercial projects are not accepted.

System Requirements

Material, Installation, and Flashing Requirements

Slate and clay tile are dimensionally variable, load-sensitive, and installation-dependent. Mismatched replacement slate, incorrect fastening gauge, or improper flashing integration can cause premature failure in materials with documented service lives of 75 to 150 years.

Copper flashing on a heritage roof is fabricated to specific profiles and thicknesses. Standard flashing products are not compatible with heritage roof assemblies. Valley geometry, chimney saddle design, and gutter expansion behavior differ from modern systems and must be matched to the original installation.

Training and certification through Ludowici Factory Install Training, TRI Alliance, the National Slate Association, and the Slate Roofing Contractors Association are system-specific and do not transfer across materials.

Our Approach

Period-Matched Materials, Documented Installation

Each project follows a consistent process for material selection, installation, and documentation.

  • Slate and tile matched to period specification. Replacement slates are sourced by quarry region, thickness, and color blend to match the existing field. Clay tile is matched by profile (S-curve, flat, English, or French) and by manufacturer where records exist.

  • Copper fabricated on-site. Valleys, flashings, chimney saddles, gutters, and ridgework are fabricated at the project location to the dimensions and profile of the existing metalwork. No off-the-shelf flashing products are used on historic roof systems.

  • Documentation for preservation and insurance records. All work is documented: materials used, source, installation method, and pre-existing conditions observed during scope. Documentation is provided in a format compatible with state and local preservation office requirements.

  • Site-specific safety plans. Slate and clay tile surfaces require specific load distribution and access controls. Each site receives a safety plan designed for the specific surface type, structural age, and any preservation restrictions on the property.

Our Commitment

We do not substitute materials without client and preservation authority approval. Where conditions on the roof differ from what was documented in scope (undiscovered damage, incompatible underlying systems, or non-original prior repairs), we stop work, document the condition, and consult with the client and any applicable preservation authority before proceeding.
