Services · J. Turner

Investigative Research & FOIA Consulting

Whether you're building a defense, filing a civil rights suit, or producing a documentary — public records and digital evidence can make or break your case.

I help attorneys, journalists, and investigators get the documents, data, and analysis that move things forward.

FOIA & Public Records

Full lifecycle management: drafting targeted requests, tracking across agencies, handling appeals, and organizing production for litigation or editorial use.

Active portfolios across 15+ federal and state agencies. I know which exemptions to challenge and how to push back when agencies stall.

Forensic Data Research & Analysis

Documentation and analysis of outputs from mobile device extractions (Cellebrite, GrayKey), Apple Health records, carrier ping data, and geofence warrants — identifying what the data shows, flagging inconsistencies, and organizing findings for attorney review.

Research and analysis work only — not expert witness testimony or certified forensic examination. If you need a testifying expert, I can help you understand the records before that engagement begins.

OSINT & Investigative Research

Open-source intelligence gathering, corporate and public records research, and background analysis for legal strategy, journalism, and documentary production.

Data Tools & Visualization

Custom tools for investigators who need more than a spreadsheet: interactive dashboards, geospatial viewers, and data pipelines built to your project.

I also develop FOIA.io, a SaaS platform for FOIA request management.

Records Organization & Production

Structuring large document productions for litigation or editorial review — indexing, deduplication, timeline building, and exhibit preparation from public records and agency responses.

Investigative Support for Ongoing Cases

Extended research partnerships for law firms and productions with active matters that need consistent records coverage — agency monitoring, follow-up requests, and interim reporting on request status and new productions.
