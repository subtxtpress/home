Petitioner Lauren Matthias (Petitioner), by and through counsel, pursuant to Rule 7(l)(1)(E) of the Utah Rules of Civil Procedure, respectfully moves this Court to continue the evidentiary hearing currently scheduled for October 16, 2025 (the “Hearing”). Under Rule  7(l)  the  Court  may  rule  on  this  motion  without  waiting  for  a  response  as  it  is  a motion for extension of time filed before the expiration of time. 

After  the  hearing  date  was  scheduled,  Petitioner  retained  Deiss  Law,  PC  as additional  counsel.  Undersigned  counsel  (“Counsel”)  entered  their  appearance  on October 10, 2025 – just six days before the scheduled evidentiary hearing. Counsel are

working diligently to get up to speed on the case, but the limited timeframe makes it difficult to thoroughly review hearing materials and prepare arguments while ensuring the  preservation  of  Petitioner's  rights  in  light  of  threats  to  Petitioner’s  safety  and wellbeing. Given the serious nature of this stalking injunction, the volume of materials to review, and the sensitive nature of the testimony required, six days is insufficient time for Counsel to ensure effective representation. 

In addition to these considerations, there has been an “Unspecified, Unverified Media Request” regarding the Hearing. This request raises significant issues regarding victim safety, privacy rights, and the protection of sensitive testimony. Petitioner has already filed an objection to this request, which is currently pending before the Court.

Whatever  the  Court's  ruling  on  this  request,  it  will  have  substantial  implications  for Petitioner’s  procedural  needs  leading  up  to  the  Hearing,  further  hindering  Counsel’s ability to adequately familiarize themselves with basic case materials. 

A  brief  continuance  will  allow  counsel  sufficient  time  to  prepare  while maintaining  the  protection  of  the  existing  Temporary  Civil  Stalking  Injunction.  This continuance  will  serve  the  interests  of  justice  and  ensure  effective  representation without substantial prejudice to any party. 

Petitioner therefore respectfully requests that the Court continue the October 16th, 2025, hearing.
