Background

Alleged Fear and Emotional Distress

43. I dispute the allegations regarding Christina M’s online posts and their alleged impact on Matthias, her husband, and Yohr.
44. It is not clear to me what posts and public comments Matthias and Yohr reference in their declarations.
45. However, I have listened to and/or viewed a significant amount of Christina M content—some of which is about Matthias.
46. The content does not threaten, defame, or harass Matthias.
47. Christina M does not purport to address Matthias directly or indirectly.
48. Rather, Christina M is simply engaging in public discourse regarding topics of interest in the true crime community, which, at times, includes Matthias and Matthias’s conduct and public statements.
49. Matthias has spoken publicly on many aspects of her personal life, her purported credentials, and a variety of true crime-related topics.
