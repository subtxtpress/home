Matthias Sends a Cease-and-Desist

9. I attended Lori Daybell’s trial in Maricopa County, Arizona in April 2025.

13.  Matthias approached me while recording me and asked if I was recording her. She later submitted the video to the Boise Police Department.
14. Matthias became emotional and ran into the courthouse, claiming that I was threatening her and bullying her.
