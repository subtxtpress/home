My name is Susan Hendricks. I am a former anchor for CNN and HLN, as well as an author. I first met Lauren Matthias in the fall of 2024 while reporting on the highly publicized double-murder trial in Delphi, Indiana.

The trial was among the most intense I have covered in my career, with no cameras allowed inside the courtroom. It demanded long days, sleepless nights on the courthouse steps, and hours spent waiting in line just to gain access. It was under these extraordinary circumstances that I became acquainted with Lauren—not only as a fellow journalist and colleague, but very quickly as a friend.

Over time, I came to know Lauren Matthias and her producer, Grayson, on a more personal level. I became familiar with Lauren’s mannerisms, her temperament, and the way she conducted herself on a daily basis.

In January, Grayson connected me to Christina M via text because I was looking for someone to help me with some graphic design work. Christina M and I sent a few texts back and forth, but nothing was ever set in stone and official.

Fast forward to April of 2025 and Lauren and I began covering the same trial in Phoenix, Arizona. On April 9th, I planned to miss the morning session of court. At some point that morning, Grayson reached out and asked me to come over to their AirBnB as soon as possible because something had happened, and Lauren was not okay.

I showed up to their AirBnB a little while later, and Lauren was in a state I had never seen her in before. Lauren was extremely emotional and told me that Christina M had confronted her in the courthouse, about calling her annoying in a text. Lauren said that her past messages with former contract employees were being sent out because they were angry at her. I re-assured Lauren that everything was going to be okay and that she would get through this.

When the social media posts started in June, the shift in Lauren became even more apparent. She became extremely fearful for her safety, even more than she already was. She would often talk to me on the phone and talk about the anxiety she was feeling, and how unsafe she felt given what was occurring.

I met up with Grayson and Lauren again in July at the Bryan Kohberger sentencing. There was a line to get in for sentencing beginning the night before, and many of the accomplices Christina M has, were in the front of that line. I recognized many of them from the social media posts and comments, as well as seeing them at different court events that they would show up for.   

Lauren and Grayson met me there to get in line with me, and ended up leaving in the middle of the night because Lauren expressed that she was feeling unsafe and intimidated by the group. It was clear that there was significant tension and Lauren left to de-escalate a situation that she did not cause. Lauren did, however, come back for the press conference where it was clear to me, she was feeling stressed and panicked.

The next morning, I got breakfast with both Lauren and Grayson before my flight home. They explained that they had to call police that night before after a confrontation occurred at dinner, by Christina M’s accomplices. They shared that after the two of them were confronted, they felt they could not safely leave the restaurant, which is why they called the police.

It was clear to me that the two of them were in an absolute panicked state and what I would describe as fight or flight. They were concerned and worried. I noticed that they kept looking at the door any time there was a noise. Lauren especially was on high alert and extremely emotional as she explained how much fear she had and how unsafe she felt with the escalation.

Lauren called me weeks later and told me that Christina M was now posting about her husband on social media and she shared with me how harmful it was to both herself and her husband. She was fearful for her family's livelihood based on the public lies being spread about her husband and his career by Christina M.

I again witnessed the fear Lauren continues to have, while at CrimeCon in Denver. Aside from my pre-committed work events during CrimeCon, I tried to stay with Lauren as much as possible throughout the duration of the convention.

Both Lauren and myself are trained and seasoned journalists. When being trained as a journalist, you are trained to be non emotional, to have thick skin and to push through things. It is extremely clear to me from conversations I have had with Lauren that she has tried to remain that way, but at this point, she no longer can. I’ve witnessed increased fear and stress in Lauren over the last several months and I have become very concerned for her and her safety as the stalking and harassment continues.
