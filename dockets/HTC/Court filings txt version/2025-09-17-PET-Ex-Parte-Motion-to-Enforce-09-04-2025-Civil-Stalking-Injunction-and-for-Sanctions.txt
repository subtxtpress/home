III. LEGAL AUTHORITY

8.  Utah Rule of Civil Procedure 7A provides the mechanism for enforcing court orders through contempt proceedings.
9.  Utah Code § 78B-7-703 states that a violation of a civil stalking injunction:
a.  Constitutes the criminal offense of stalking under Utah Code § 76-5-106.5
b.  May be enforced by a civil action initiated by the petitioner
c.  May be enforced by a criminal action initiated by a prosecuting attorney
10. This Court has inherent authority to enforce its orders through contempt proceedings and to impose appropriate sanctions for violations.
