Order Finding Motion Moot.
The Appellant, Zachary Rye Adams, is appealing the denial of his post-conviction petition. He filed notice of appeal on July 22, 2026. The record has not been filed, but the deadline for doing so has not yet expired. Tenn. R. App. P. 24 and 25.
Currently before the Court is the State’s motion for a limited remand to the trial court so it may “seek a protective order” for “certain information [previously ordered by the trial court] to be sealed or confidential.” In its motion, the State alleges “a third party – who gained access to the unredacted discovery while employed as the appellant’s agent – is posting sealed and confidential information on social media and disseminating it via a podcast.” The Appellant opposes. Counsel for the Appellant asserts “[r]emand is unnecessary because the orders the State needs already exist.” Furthermore, counsel insists they are not responsible for the action of a non-party and that they will continue to abide by the directives of the trial court’s protective orders and the Rules of Professional Conduct.
Generally, and as both parties recognize, the jurisdiction of this Court attaches to the case after notice of appeal is filed. See State v. Peele, 58 S.W.3d 701, 705 (Tenn. 2001). However, this Court has the authority to remand matters to the trial court for consideration in certain situations. See, e.g., Tenn. R. App. P. 18, Advisory Commission Comment [2006] (appellate court not precluded from remanding matter to trial court for indigency determination); Tenn. R. App. P. 24(e) (“Any differences regarding whether the record accurately discloses what occurred in the trial court shall be submitted to and settled by the trial court regardless of whether the record has been transmitted to the appellate court.”); Boutrous v. State, No. M2025-01076-CCA-R3-PC, 2026 WL 1804180 (Tenn. Crim. App. June 23, 2026) (Order, Sep. 11, 2025) (remanding matter to trial court for waiver of counsel determination).
That being said, and despite the fact that notice of appeal is on file from the final order in this post-conviction matter, the trial court has not lost the authority to address concerns regarding any prior order it entered regarding the confidentiality of filings in this post-conviction matter. Thus, a remand, or a stay of proceedings on appeal, is not necessary. The State’s motion is, therefore, denied as moot. This appeal shall now continue in accordance with the Rules of Appellate Procedure. Any subsequent order entered by the trial court on the issue of confidentiality of filings in this case may be included in the appellate record, if necessary.

Motion for Extension to File Statement/Transcript of Evidence filed by Adams, Zachary Rye (Appellant/Petitioner).
10-21-2026
Order Related to Extension Granted.
This matter is before the Court upon the Appellant’s motion for additional time to file the transcript of evidence. The Appellant filed notice of appeal on July 22, 2026, but he did not file the instant motion until October 20, 2026. Tenn. R. App. P. 24(b) (transcript due sixty days after notice of appeal filed). The Appellant is requesting until January 19, 2027, to file the complete transcript with the trial court clerk. He indicates the State is unopposed to a thirty-day extension at this time. And although the Appellant suggests the requested extension is at the behest of the court reporters, he failed to attach to his motion their affidavits attesting to the reasons for that request. Tenn. Ct. Crim. App. R. 8, Comments.
The Court recognizes the extent of the proceedings conducted in this post-conviction matter. The Appellant states a portion of those proceedings have already been transcribed. Upon review, the Court hereby grants the requested extension, in part. Accordingly, the Appellant shall now have until December 18, 2026, to file the complete transcript with the trial court clerk. Thereafter, this matter shall continue to proceed in accordance with the Rules of Appellate Procedure. Tenn. R. App. P. 24 and 25 (record due on appeal forty-five days after transcript filed). Should the Appellant require additional time beyond this deadline, he shall include in his motion the affidavits of the reporters attesting to the reason(s) for the request.
