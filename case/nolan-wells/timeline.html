July 4, 2026
Nolan Wells

An 18-year-old wide receiver celebrates Independence Day on the water with friends. What unfolds over 48 hours becomes a mystery that grips the nation.

9:56 AM · July 4
Boats Depart

Three boats leave Fort Bayou Launch carrying ~20–30 people. Nolan boards Wyatt's Triton. Warren travels on Bart's Tide Line.

10:30 AM · July 4
Horn Island

Boats arrive at Horn Island northwestern tip. Approximately 2,000+ people. Three rows of boats stretching miles along the shoreline.

~2:00–4:00 PM · July 4
The Day Unfolds

Nolan meets a girl named Katie. They spend the afternoon together. Around 3 PM, Warren last sees them laughing.

~4:15 PM · July 4
Bilge Pump Fails

Warren checks on the Triton. Water up to the batteries. The bilge pump isn't working. The boat is sinking.

4:25–4:30 PM · July 4
Nolan Stays Behind

Warren shouts: "Get on the boat! We're leaving!"

Nolan: "No, I'm staying out here. I'm going to ride in with somebody else."

Katie is there and will later confirm she heard them asking Nolan to board.

4:30 PM · July 4
Emergency Return

Damaged Triton is towed to Fort Bayou. Nolan remains on the island with Katie. Still ~2,000 people there.

~11:00 PM · July 4
Something's Wrong

Warren can't reach Nolan. Calls Coast Guard. Nolan's parents go to Warren's Home searching for answers.

6:00 AM · July 5
Search & Rescue

Multi-agency search. United Cajun Navy, Coast Guard, helicopters, planes. Lake Mars Pier becomes command center.

8:40 AM · July 6
Body Found

A U.S. Park Service ranger discovers a body face down at the western tip of Horn Island—where the boats were anchored.

Later that day · July 6
Identification

Family taken to Coroner's Office in Pascagoula. Body confirmed via dental records.

July 7–23, 2026
Investigation Underway

Warren and friends cooperate fully. Ben Crump retained. Independent autopsy ordered. Cause of death: undetermined, pending investigation.
