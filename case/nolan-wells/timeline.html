July 3–6, 2026
Nolan Wells

An 18-year-old wide receiver vanished from a crowded barrier island on July 4, 2026. Two days later, his body washed ashore. This is the timeline of conflicting accounts, unanswered questions, and an ongoing investigation into the circumstances surrounding his death.

Evening · July 3
Last Dinner

Nolan made salmon dinner for his parents. Before leaving to spend the night at Warren's house, he hugged his mom. "Hey mom, I'm about to go. I love you," he said. She hugged him back. It was the last time she saw him alive.

Early Morning · July 4
Fourth of July Plans

Nolan, Warren, and friends headed to Horn Island, a federally protected barrier island known for its untouched beaches and isolation. Approximately 2,000+ people were expected there for the holiday weekend.

9:56 AM · July 4
Boats Depart

Three boats left from different docks near Ocean Springs, carrying ~20 to 30 people total. Nolan and Warren rode together on one of the boats. Jayvon Williams, a close friend, was going to ride with Nolan but that boat filled up, so he went on a different vessel.

11:14 AM · July 4
Horn Island Arrival

The group's boats arrived at the northwestern tip of Horn Island. Three rows of boats stretched for miles along the shoreline. The beach was packed. Jayvon saw Nolan after they arrived. He told ABC News: "As soon as we got out there, Nolan told me that he loved me."

~2:00–3:00 PM · July 4
Meeting Katie

Nolan met a girl named Katie. They spent the afternoon together on the beach. Around 3 PM, Warren last saw Nolan with their friends, and they appeared to be laughing.

~4:00 PM · July 4
Altercation on Island

A video showing someone yelling in a heated argument: "Give me my f-ing phone back." Many initially believed it was Nolan in the video. Later, Tracestin Shepherd, a friend on the trip, clarified: "That's me yelling. That's the video I am referring to." Jayvon confirmed: "We were calming Tracestin down in that video." Katie reportedly said she last saw Nolan around this time, still with his friends.

~4:15 PM · July 4
Bilge Pump Fails

Warren checked on the Triton. Water was up to the batteries. The bilge pump wasn't working and the boat was taking on water. A private towing company was called to report the emergency.

4:25–4:30 PM · July 4
Nolan Stays Behind

Warren told Nolan: "Get on the boat! We're leaving!" Nolan responded: "No, I'm staying out here. I'm going to ride in with somebody else." Katie will later, reportedly, confirm she heard them asking Nolan to board. But Nolan stayed on the island—the only one from his original boat to do so.

4:30 PM · July 4
Emergency Return

The damaged Triton was towed to the Fort Bayou Boat Launch. Nolan remained on the island with Katie and ~2,000 other people. At this time, no one knew this would be the last time anyone saw him alive.

~11:00 PM · July 4
Friends Call—He's Not Back

One of Nolan's friends called his mother to say Nolan still hadn't returned from the island. His mother called Tashema Hands, mother of Jayvon Williams, to ask if she'd seen him. Jayvon and his brother check Nolan's Snapchat location. His mother checks Life360. The data doesn't match.

11:00 PM+ · July 4
Locating the Phone

Tashema Hands used Snapchat to track Nolan's phone to a house. She finds his phone there with the friends who had been with the group that day. It takes pressing them before they admit they have it. When Nolan's mother finally gets the phone and scrolls through it, she notices something disturbing: there are zero saved photos or videos from the boat or the island.

Late Night · July 4
Where Are the Keys?

Nolan's parents arrive at Warren's house to get his vehicle and search for him. Several friends answer the door. It's tense. One friend who was asleep comes out and says he doesn't know where the keys are. After 15 minutes of searching, they still can't find them. Then Nolan's father overhears one of the boys on the phone with a girl, who says the keys are on the XBox. About 5 minutes later, they find them.

~11:30 PM–12:00 AM · July 4–5
Missing Person Report

Nolan's mother calls Ocean Springs Police to report him missing. They tell her to contact the Department of Marine Resources (DMR) because the incident happened on the island, which is federal land. When she calls DMR, she's told she needs to file with Ocean Springs Police first. Eventually, someone from DMR starts the missing person report to avoid further delay.

12:00 AM · July 5
Meet with DMR

Nolan's parents meet with an officer from DMR at a McDonald's parking lot to provide details for the missing person report. One of Nolan's friends also contacts the Coast Guard directly to report him missing.

2:53 AM · July 5
Photos Surface

Evan Noel posts a photo on Instagram showing Nolan in the pool at the party. The image will later become central to increased speculation about what happened before someone clarifies the pool party occured on June 27th, not July 4th. Around this time, the friends' lawyers advise them to stop cooperating and retain legal representation.

6:00 AM · July 5
Private Search Begins

Nolan's coach, who saw social media posts about him missing, contacts Nolan's father on Facebook and offers to take him out on his boat. They go to Horn Island to search. Nolan's father sees a family in a tent that appears to be living on the island.

12:26 PM · July 5
Official Search Launches

The U.S. Coast Guard Sector Mobile receives a request for assistance from Jackson County Sheriff's Office. An urgent marine information broadcast is issued. A helicopter is sent to search the area. Multiple agencies mobilize: Coast Guard, Mississippi Department of Marine Resources, U.S. National Park Service, Gulf Islands National Seashore.

5:54 PM · July 5
Command Post Established

Jackson County Sheriff's Office establishes a command post at Lake Mars Boat Launch. Sheriff John Ledbetter coordinates with multiple agencies. The United Cajun Navy, a nonprofit volunteer search and rescue group from Louisiana, joins the effort. Their vice president tells CNN: if someone fell in the water, strong rip currents that day would have posed a serious risk.

6:00 AM · July 6
UCN Continues Search

The United Cajun Navy begins searching via land, air, and sea. Search boats are deployed at 8:00 AM. The last vessels leave just before 8 AM to continue the search for Nolan.

8:40 AM · July 6
Body Found

A U.S. Park Service ranger discovers a body face down in the water at the western tip of Horn Island where the boats had been anchored. The body matches Nolan's description: 6'1", approximately 180 pounds, wearing blue swim trunks.

8:45 AM+ · July 6
Family Notified

Sheriff Ledbetter confirms authorities received a call reporting a body found at the northwestern tip of Horn Island. Nolan's family is asked to come to Lake Mars Boat Launch, where they meet with Sheriff Ledbetter and Coroner Bruce Lynd. There are no immediate, obvious signs of foul play or trauma on the body.

11:00 AM · July 6
Identification

The family is taken to the Coroner's Office in Pascagoula. The body is confirmed via dental records. It is Nolan Wells.

1:00 PM · July 6
Public Confirmation

Nolan's family confirms his death on social media. His mother posts on Facebook: the body found on Horn Island was their son. The coroner's office requests the autopsy be conducted at the state medical examiner's office due to the "condition" of the body and to determine if there was any trauma or foul play.

July 7, 2026
Legal Team Retained

The Wells family retains nationally renowned civil rights attorney Ben Crump. Crump calls for a "thorough and transparent investigation." Crump tells ABC News: the family is concerned about South Mississippi law enforcement investigating a case where a "young Black man" may have been involved with "young white students who may have some culpability."

July 7, 2026
Independent Autopsy Ordered

The family's legal team orders an independent autopsy. Nolan's body is flown to Washington, D.C., for examination by experienced forensic pathologist Dr. Roger Mitchell. The state medical examiner conducts an autopsy in Mississippi. Cause of death: pending investigation. Date of death: unknown pending investigation.

July 22, 2026
Autopsy Findings

At the NAACP National Convention, Ben Crump announces preliminary findings from the independent autopsy. Cause and manner of death: undetermined "pending investigation." The second autopsy revealed: no lacerations of skin or fractures of skull, but a 12-by-8-inch area of red discoloration in soft tissue on the back of Nolan's head. The throat was not received with the body, making it impossible to rule out injury to the neck as a cause of death.

July 23, 2026 · Present
Investigation Continues

Over 60 witnesses have been interviewed. The FBI is assisting with digital evidence. District Attorney Angel Myers McIlrath confirmed the state medical examiner's autopsy report will be withheld until a grand jury reviews it. Rewards totaling $125,000 have been offered. Critical questions remain unanswered: What were the circumstances that led to Nolan entering the water? Was there water in the airway? What are the toxicology findings? Investigators await answers.
