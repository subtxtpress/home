July 4, 2026 — Morning Prep

Saturday
Early AM
Warren wakes up at his buddy's house, grabs breakfast at McDonald's. Checks on his boat (hooked to his truck) and fills it with ice.
Morning
Three boats are prepared and loaded:
Wyatt's 22-foot Triton Nolan (outbound only — stays on island), Warren (return only — moved from Palm Beach area to Triton during distress). ~8–9 people total outbound.

Morning

July 4, 2026 — Horn Island

Saturday · 9:56 AM – 4:30 PM
9:56 AM
Boats depart from El Camino Real Rd. (private launch in Ocean Springs area).
~10:30 AM
Boats arrive at Horn Island (northwestern tip). Island is packed: approximately 2,000–3,000+ people, three rows of boats stretching miles along the shoreline. The west tip (where the group anchors) is the most popular spot.
Conditions: Clear skies, calm water (3–4 knots), excellent visibility. Great day to be on the water.
~10:30 AM onward
Boats tie up; the group settles in for the day. People get in the water, drinks and food are distributed. Nolan seems "completely fine" in conversations with Warren, Jayvon, and others. No one tracking him specifically due to the large crowd.
~2:00–2:30 PM
Nolan meets a girl named Katie on the island. They spend time together throughout the afternoon. Tracestin Shepherd (a friend on the trip) described Nolan as staying with her: "They met that day...he really did like her."
~3:00 PM
Warren last sees Nolan: "The last time I saw Nolan was three o'clock. Nolan was cradling Katie in his arms...Nolan was shaking his head, laughing, and I was like, 'Dude, you crazy.'" Katie is confirmed as a witness to all subsequent events.
~3:50 PM
Tracestin Shepherd and his girlfriend are walking back toward his uncle's boat when Nolan crosses their path, heading the other direction toward Wyatt's Triton. They dap up. Tracestin makes a joking comment about the girl Nolan is with. His last words to Nolan: "All right, buddy. I love you. See you later." — "I'm glad I said it," Tracestin later told interviewers. "That was my last encounter with Nolan."
~4:00 PM
A video circulates showing someone shouting "Give me my f-ing phone back." Many initially assumed it was Nolan. Tracestin clarified: "That's me yelling." The incident escalated into a physical altercation: a stranger interjected himself into an argument between Tracestin and his girlfriend over a dropped koozie lid. Tracestin told him twice to step away. The stranger threw the first punch. Tracestin's uncle pulled him onto the boat. Carter (Warren's younger brother) then confronted the stranger verbally. Tracestin: "I was the only Black person involved. The stranger was white, Carter was white." Nolan was not involved — he was with Katie elsewhere.
Note: Ben Crump later claimed the person in the video was Nolan. Tracestin responded: "Nice try — but there was another Black guy on the island. Now your whole narrative needs to change again."
Tracestin left the island 30–45 minutes after the fight.
~4:00–4:15 PM
Separately, Warren's younger brother gets into a fight on the beach. Warren is alerted and runs to help. Fight is broken up; brother was angry but okay. Nolan was not involved.
~4:15–4:20 PM
Warren checks the bilge hatch on Wyatt's Triton: water up to the batteries. Motor won't respond. Wyatt throws a rear anchor; crew begins emergency procedures. Bart (on the Tide Line) offers to tow them out of the chaos.
~4:25 PM
Wyatt and crew begin shouting for people to get off the boat or get on the tow. They yell for Nolan: "Nolan, get on the boat! We're leaving! The boat's having issues!" Multiple calls. Nolan responds: "No, I'm staying out here. I'm going to ride in with somebody else."
Wyatt asks multiple times. This is not uncommon — people frequently ride out on one boat and return on another.
Katie is present and texts a friend the next day: "The last time I saw him was when they were yelling for him to get on the boat." WITNESS
4:30 PM
Boats depart Horn Island with the damaged Triton being towed by Bart's Tide Line. Nolan remains on the island with Katie. Multiple people (at least 5–8 on the Triton + others) heard Nolan refuse to board. The Palm Beach had already left earlier.
Note: ~20–30 people still on the island at this time, plus 100s more from other groups.
~4:30–5:30 PM
Nolan is last reported seen on the island by someone around 6:30 PM (per one account). No confirmed sightings between 4:30 and that time. He was with Katie.

July 4, 2026 — Return & Evening

Saturday · 4:30 PM onward
~5:52 PM
Damaged Triton is towed into Fort Bayou. Wyatt and crew jump in water to reinstall the drain plug. Boat is planed off (40+ mph) to expel excess water.
~6:06 PM
Boats dock (original location). The group heads to Bart's grandparents' house to eat and rest after a long day.
~7:30 PM
Warren and friends transition to an evening event: Fourth of July fireworks and pool party at a girlfriend's house. They swim, hang out, celebrate.
~9:00–10:00 PM
At the pool party, Warren talks to buddy Wyatt and others. Warren asks: "Where's Nolan? Have y'all heard from him?" No one has. Mildly concerning.
~9:20 PM
Warren calls Tracestin. Tracestin is asleep on his uncle's boat — tired from the day — and doesn't answer.
~11:00 PM
Warren texts Jayvon asking if Nolan rode back with him. Jayvon says no. Warren requests Jayvon's mother's phone number (Tashema Hands). Says: "If she hasn't heard from Nolan, I'm calling the Coast Guard."
~11:00 PM
Tracestin's brother wakes him: "Warren's trying to get a hold of you. They're looking for Nolan." Tracestin calls Warren back on a mutual friend's phone. Warren is on speakerphone with others in the room and is already dialing the Coast Guard. Tracestin's initial read: "He's probably just out somewhere with that girl — get a hold of the girl, you'll find Nolan." Warren: "We're starting to freak out." Warren hangs up to call the Coast Guard. That is their last contact until the next morning.
~11:00 PM
Warren calls Christine Wonsley, Nolan's mother. First call she receives that night about Nolan being missing. Warren: "Don't freak out. I'm going to call the Coast Guard."

July 4–5, 2026 — Night & Initial Response

Late Saturday / Early Sunday
~11:00 PM
Warren calls the U.S. Coast Guard and speaks for ~20 minutes. Provides: description of Nolan, what he was wearing, last known location, time last seen, boat info, water conditions, and context. Warren's reasoning: "People die out there all the time. I'd rather call 100 times and he be okay than not know where he is."
12:00 AM
Christine Wonsley and Elmore Wonsley (Nolan's parents) meet a DMR officer at a McDonald's parking lot to officially file a missing person report. Coast Guard is also contacted.
After midnight
Warren's parents retrieve Nolan's car keys from Warren's house (they were left on an Xbox). After ~15 minutes of searching. Nolan's parents use Life360 and Snapchat to track Nolan's phone — data conflicts between apps. Friends are reluctant to produce the phone initially; Tashema Hands retrieves it from a house (~15 min process). No photos or videos from the island are on the phone.

July 5, 2026

Sunday
Early AM
Christine Wonsley posts social media appeals. States family has Nolan's phone; they've traveled to the island to search. Posts photos of Nolan from July 4 (blue swim trunks, no shirt, sunglasses).
Early AM
Friends on the island trip begin to lawyer up (specific individuals not yet publicly named, but Warren cooperates fully without an attorney).
Before 6 AM
Nolan's football coach sees social media posts and contacts Elmore Wonsley on Facebook, offering his boat for searching.
6:00 AM
Elmore Wonsley and the coach head out on the coach's boat to Horn Island. Elmore observes a family camping/living in a tent on the island.
~7:00 AM
Tracestin wakes early and goes back out on Wyatt's boat — still in the water — with Wyatt and others to search around Horn Island.
9:45 AM–Noon
Tracestin, Wyatt, and others search the waters around Horn Island. A rescue official tells them the professionals have the area under control and directs them to use social media instead. They return to shore and spend the rest of Sunday and into the night sharing posts and trying to reach anyone who may have seen or spoken to Nolan after 4:30 PM.
12:26 PM
Coast Guard Sector Mobile receives request from JCSO for assistance. Urgent marine broadcast issued; helicopter deployed.
12:31 PM
JCSO press release: Searching for Nolan Xavier Wells, 18, Black male, 6′1″, 180 lbs. Last seen July 4 on Horn Island in blue swim trunks, no shirt, sunglasses.
5:54 PM
JCSO establishes command post at Lake Mars Boat Launch (1200 Lake Mars Ave, Ocean Springs). Multi-agency search underway: Gulf Islands National Seashore, NPS, USCG, MDMR, MS Dept. of Wildlife/Fisheries/Parks, Biloxi Fire, Harrison County SO, Jackson County Fire, United Cajun Navy.

July 6, 2026

Monday
~5:00 AM
Tracestin is up at 5:00 AM. The plan: take another boat out to the Chandelier Islands at 7:00 AM to expand the search area. He fuels his boat at 6:00 AM, puts it on the lift, and stops at McDonald's first.
~6:00 AM
While leaving McDonald's, Tracestin gets the news: Nolan's body has been found. "I wasn't there to help him in his last minutes," he later said. "But now I do everything in my power to help." He donated to the family, reached out personally, and has since given multiple on-record interviews to address what he describes as false narratives about his friend group.

United Cajun Navy launches coordinated search via land, air, and sea.
~8:00 AM
Search boats deployed; last vessels leave just before 8 AM.
8:40 AM

8:45 AM
Sheriff Ledbetter confirms authorities received a call reporting the body. Coroner Bruce Lynd is notified. No immediate signs of physical injury or obvious foul play noted. Family is called to Lake Mars Boat Launch.
11:00 AM
Family taken to coroner's office in Pascagoula for identification.
1:00 PM
Christine Wonsley posts on Facebook confirming the body is Nolan.
Afternoon
Coroner Lynd confirms body via dental records. Requests state autopsy due to uncertain circumstances and body condition.

Tuesday

Nolan "made a decision to stay on the island and return inland later with another group of friends." Warren was interviewed by JCSO and cooperated fully. She and her husband were not at Horn Island. Emphasizes respect for the Wells family and community.

July 15, 2026

Wednesday
Morning
Wells family and attorneys Ben Crump and Eric Hertz meet with DA Angel Myers McIlrath. Family agrees to "mutual inspection" of Nolan's phone by experts from both parties. Crump: Case will go to grand jury after JCSO investigation concludes (standard procedure).
Afternoon
Press conference at Jackson County Courthouse. Funeral announced for July 20 at Center Pointe Church, Ocean Springs. Visitation 9–11 AM; service afterward. Celebration of life at Jackson County Fairgrounds.
Afternoon
Sheriff Ledbetter confirms FBI is assisting with digital evidence examination. Over 60 witnesses have been interviewed, including the friends on the boat. Death threats reported by Ashlee Cole and Tracestin Shepherd; FBI is "working to address interstate threats."

July 22, 2026

Wednesday · NAACP National Convention, Chicago
8:00 AM
Ben Crump presents preliminary independent autopsy findings (conducted by Dr. Roger Mitchell, Washington D.C., July 10):
  • Cause & manner of death: Undetermined, pending investigation
  • Date of death: Unknown
  • Physical findings: Heavy decomposition; no lacerations, no skull fractures
  • Key finding: 12-by-8-inch area of red discoloration in soft tissue on back of head
  • Missing: Throat not received; organs removed (noted as routine for testing)
  • Unable to rule out: Neck injury, intracranial hemorrhage, water in airway
  • Toxicology: Original results not received at time of report
  • Official autopsy: State Medical Examiner's report still pending (per Coroner Bruce Lynd Jr.); will be withheld until grand jury review
Crump: "With suspicion of drowning, it is important to confirm how that person entered the water... At the time of this report, this forensic pathologist does not know the condition of Nolan Wells. He did not know whether he was conscious or unconscious when he entered the water — he said that would be significant. He did not know whether he was forced into the water or unforced — he said that would be significant. The condition of Nolan Wells before he entered the water would be the crux of the matter."
Christine Wonsley: "Losing a child, it truly breaks your heart. It is a pain that is, just, you can't measure it. Especially someone like Nolan, who brought so much joy and light and life to everyone. The only question we have is, what happened to our son?"
Outstanding questions: Circumstances of entering water? Was Nolan conscious? Forced or unforced entry? Water in airway? Evidence of neck injuries? Stomach contents? Toxicology results?
