July 3–4, 2026
Nolan Wells

An 18-year-old wide receiver from Ocean Springs, Mississippi, plans to celebrate Independence Day on the water with friends. What unfolds over the next 48 hours will become a mystery that grips the nation.

9:56 AM · July 4
Boats Depart

Three boats leave Fort Bayou Launch carrying ~20–30 people. Nolan boards Wyatt's Triton. Warren travels on Bart's Tide Line.

10:30 AM · July 4
Horn Island Arrival

Boats arrive at Horn Island northwestern tip. ~2,000+ people, three rows of boats. Nolan gets in the water, seems fine.

~2:00–4:00 PM · July 4
Island Hours

Nolan meets Katie. They spend the afternoon together. Around 3 PM, Warren last sees them laughing.

~4:15 PM · July 4
Emergency

Triton's bilge pump fails. Water up to batteries. Warren sees it filling up. Panic. Bart offers to tow them out.

4:25–4:30 PM · July 4
Nolan Stays Behind

Warren shouts for Nolan: "Get on the boat! We're leaving!"

Nolan: "No, I'm staying out here. I'm going to ride in with somebody else."

4:30 PM · July 4

Damaged Triton is towed to Fort Bayou. Nolan remains on island with Katie. ~2,000 people still there.

~11:00 PM · July 4

Warren can't reach Nolan. Calls Coast Guard. Nolan's parents go to Warren's Home to get his keys. Friends reluctant to produce phone.

6:00 AM · July 5

Multi-agency search. United Cajun Navy, Coast Guard, helicopters, planes. Lake Mars Pier becomes command center.

8:40 AM · July 6
Body Found

U.S. Park Service ranger discovers a body face down at the northwestern tip of Horn Island. Same area where boats were anchored.

Later that day · July 6
Identification

Family taken to Coroner's Office in Pascagoula. Body confirmed via dental records.

July 7–23, 2026

Warren and friends cooperate fully. Ben Crump retained. Independent autopsy ordered. Cause of death: undetermined.
