July 4, 2026
What Happened to Nolan Wells?

Nolan Xavier Wells was 18 years old when he disappeared from the western tip of Horn Island on July 4, 2026. His body was found there six days later.

This timeline chronicles all known confirmed facts in the order events occurred—not the order they were reported. Sources: eyewitness accounts, GPS data, MDMR Marine Patrol Report MP2607-0016, independent autopsy, and public records.
