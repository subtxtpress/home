2022 · Executive Foundation
January 24, 2022
Andrew Singer
Chief Commercial Officer
Previously held senior commercial roles at BODE Technology. Othram's first major commercial-side hire, signaling a push beyond forensic lab services toward scalable business development.
Background
Singer's appointment marks the beginning of Othram's institutional commercial strategy. He later transitions into law enforcement services leadership as the company scales (see 2025). COMMERCIAL
January 26, 2022
Brendan Belair
Chief Strategy Officer
Capitol Hill veteran; former Staff Director and Chief Counsel for the House Judiciary Committee. His arrival signals Othram's entry into federal advocacy, DOJ engagement, and legislative influence.
Background
Belair's background on the House Judiciary Committee gives Othram direct access to the legislative networks governing forensic DNA standards and federal law enforcement funding. STRATEGY FEDERAL
August 22, 2022
Carla Davis
Chief Genetic Genealogist
Self-taught genealogist and longtime Othram donor who became the company's first Chief Genetic Genealogist — a donor-to-executive arc compressed into roughly five months.
Background
Profiled in the New York Times (March 27, 2022) as a major donor who had given $100,000+ to fund cold cases through DNASolves, primarily in Mississippi, her birth state. A nutrition consultant living in Dubai, she taught herself genealogical research through consumer DNA testing and Facebook groups like DNA Detectives, ultimately helping over 200 people identify unknown parents as a volunteer "search angel." She worked the Talladega Superspeedway Jane Doe case as a civilian volunteer embedded in the Lincoln, Alabama police task force — identifying the victim as Jean Ponders, a retired biology teacher missing since 2013. Appointed to the formal role five months after the NYT piece ran. SCIENCE NYT PROFILE
2023 · (No Executive Hires on Record)
2023
Consolidation Year
No executive appointments surfaced in public announcements. 2024 · Scientific & Investigative Credibility
April 15, 2024
Paul Holes
Forensic Investigator
One of the most recognizable cold-case investigators in the U.S., known nationally for his role in the Golden State Killer identification. His arrival gives Othram high-visibility investigative credibility and media reach.
Background
27+ years in forensic science; served with the Contra Costa County Sheriff and DA's offices and as an FBI Task Force Officer. Known from Dateline, America's Most Wanted, and The DNA of Murder podcast. Note: Holes has no confirmed connection to the JonBenét Ramsey case. FORENSIC MEDIA PRESENCE
May 21, 2024
Dr. Bruce Budowle
Senior Scientific & Operational Advisor
Arguably Othram's most significant scientific hire. Budowle helped build the modern U.S. forensic DNA system — 26 years at the FBI Laboratory, 13 years at the Center for Human Identification. His role strengthens Othram's standards compliance and scientific legitimacy.
Background
40+ years in forensic DNA; former FBI Lab senior scientist; deep involvement in DNA Advisory Board and national forensic standards committees. His presence validates Othram's methods for peer review, federal agency adoption, and courtroom admissibility. SCIENCE FBI
2025 · Commercial Expansion & Globalization
June 12, 2025
Leadership Cluster — Four Appointments
Commercial Expansion Wave
A coordinated set of hires and promotions marking Othram's pivot from elite forensic lab to scaled forensic infrastructure company. All four announced in a single release. EXPANSION
Richard Price — Chief Commercial Officer
Danny Hall — Senior Director, Commercial Operations (North America)
Nicola Oldroyd Clark — Senior Advisor, International Expansion
Andrew Singer — Vice President, Law Enforcement Services (promoted from CCO)
Strategic context
Singer's promotion from CCO to VP of Law Enforcement Services is the clearest signal: Othram is separating its commercial track (now led by Price) from its agency-facing track. Oldroyd Clark's international advisory role is the first indication of a formal global expansion strategy. GLOBAL
2025 (CrimeCon 2025)
Steve Ainsworth
Law Enforcement Liaison
30+ years with the Boulder County Sheriff's Office Investigations Section; 2,500+ death investigations across homicide, suspicious death, and general death. Consults with law enforcement agencies, coroners, and medical examiners' offices on unsolved violent crimes and unidentified human remains cases.
Background
Instructor at the Colorado Coroners Association, Wyoming Coroners Association, and Indiana State Coroners Association. Led in-service training for the Boulder County Sheriff's Office, Colorado Sheriffs Training Institute, and the LEEPAC group on death, robbery, and assault investigations. Member of the Colorado Association of Homicide Investigators, International Association of Homicide Investigators, and the Colorado Cold Case Homicide Review Team. Earliest confirmed Othram affiliation: CrimeCon 2025 speaker listing. LAW ENFORCEMENT CRIMECON 2025
