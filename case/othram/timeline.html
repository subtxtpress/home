2025 · Product Ecosystem & Federal Expansion
February 20, 2025
Bureau of Indian Affairs Partnership: Operation Spirit Return
Federal partnership for missing/unidentified American Indian/Alaska Native persons
U.S. Bureau of Indian Affairs, Office of Justice Services. Multi-case, national-level initiative to identify unknown human remains believed to belong to American Indian or Alaska Native persons. Collaborative with NamUs. Expands federal footprint to Interior Department. BIA PARTNERSHIP
May 2025
Othram Maps Launched
Graph-powered platform for forensic intelligence. Transforms GEDCOM files into dynamic, searchable, and editable family trees. Features AI-driven insights, graph-based analysis, and genetic context for genealogical investigation. PRODUCT LAUNCH
June 12, 2025
SNPSuite Product Launch
Offline, secure SNP-based analysis platform
Built on Othram's proprietary KinSNP® engine. Designed for air-gapped forensic environments. Compatible with sequencing and microarray data. Enables external labs to conduct kinship testing without internet access or proprietary software licensing.
PRODUCT LAUNCH
June 12, 2025
Major Technology Expansion & Global Initiative
Othram expands forensic technology offerings to strengthen infrastructure of justice. Deployable AI-powered software workflows, expanded commercial leadership, and global initiatives mark next phase in mission to deliver justice for everyone, everywhere. EXPANSION
July 2025
Total TreeCall Launched
Microtasking platform announced by genealogist Megan Smolenyak. Users get paid to research and build family trees on phone or computer using public records. Enables crowdsourced genealogical research for cold case investigations. PLATFORM LAUNCH

Leadership Expansion

U.S. Marine Corps Partnership Awarded
Marine Corps Installations National Capital Region contract
Selected by MCINCR-RCO to provide comprehensive genealogical support services for Marine and Family Programs Division. Scope: Identify, locate, and engage next-of-kin of unaccounted-for Marines for repatriation and historical case resolution. Capacity: Thousands of cases. Services include end-to-end genealogical research, trauma-informed next-of-kin outreach, family tree reconstruction, DNA kit assembly. Uses Othram Maps and Othram Glimpse AI tools. Credentialed genealogists (ICAPGen certified). USMC CONTRACT
