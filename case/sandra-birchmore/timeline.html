Early 2016
Sandra Meets William Killroy at Massasoit Community College
In early January 2016 Sandra Birchmore, then approximately 18-19, meets Will Killroy at the Massasoit Community College student center where he works. She comes in tired, asks to nap before class; he allows it. They become friends, then date briefly for a couple of months. During this period Sandra's mother dies (June 2016) and her grandmother passes shortly after. Sandra, at 18, makes the decision to take her mother off life support. She and her grandmother had been "two peas in a pod." After the breakup the relationship shifts to a physical-only arrangement; they continue seeing each other through approximately 2018. Killroy, Day 14
~2017
Sandra's Self-Harm Pros/Cons List; Suicidal Ideation; Killroy Threat
During the summer of 2017, at roughly age 19-20 and in the wake of losing her mother and grandmother, Sandra writes a multi-page document about self-harm. Killroy is present and helps her work through it, adding "I'm wonderful and amazing" at the bottom of her reasons-not-to page. Sandra's stated ideation is wanting to "join her grandmother, which is understandable under the circumstances" -- she does not make a direct plan. She threatens to take her life and to write a note listing Killroy as one of the reasons. Killroy testified on cross that he was never concerned she would actually act on it and never had to stop her; Sandra never attempted during the time he knew her. Sandra starts therapy. Killroy separately discloses: Sandra told him her father once attempted to sell her as a child to cover drug debts. Ex. (pros/cons list, 2017) Killroy direct/cross, Day 14
Defense vs. government framing
  • Defense (Stevens) introduced the list and Killroy's account to suggest Sandra had a documented history of suicidal ideation and past threats.
  • Government (Fogerty) cross-examined to establish the threat was specifically ~2017 -- not 2019, 2020, or 2021 -- and that Sandra never attempted during the entire period Killroy knew her. Killroy confirmed: "She was strong, a fighter, resilient and someone who always pull through."
  • Redirect: government introduced that Killroy himself wrote a statement threatening to commit suicide and blame Sandra specifically -- during the breakup.
~2018
Pregnancy/Miscarriage; Killroy Stops Contact
Sandra tells Killroy she had a miscarriage and that the baby was his. She had previously told him she was going to take her life and write a note listing him as a reason. After learning of the miscarriage, Killroy stops seeing Sandra; he does not see her after 2018. Killroy direct, Day 14
