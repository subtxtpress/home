Act 4 · POST Decertification / 2022 – 2024
03/21/2024
POST Voluntary Decertification Agreement: Farwell Permanently Decertified
Peace Officer Standards and Training (POST) Commission issues a Voluntary Decertification Agreement permanently revoking Farwell's Massachusetts law enforcement certification. Entry made in the National Decertification Index (NDI). Farwell waived all rights to contest. Fanning FOIAFlagged
Patrick Hanley, the same attorney who refused DNA and polygraph in March 2021, calling them "far outside the norm for mere witnesses", is listed as Respondent's attorney in the POST proceeding. The March 2021 refusal effectively delayed the investigation; the 2024 agreement closed Farwell's certification permanently without any admission. Hanley's continuity across both proceedings is documented in this production.
Act 5 · Pre-Trial Records Activity / Sept 2024 – April 2025
09/09 to 09/10/2024
Multiple PRRs: Globe Asks Who Told Farwell About the Jan. 20 Call
Multiple new PRRs received at NDAO and MSP. The Globe specifically requests: (1) audio of the Jan. 20, 2021 call from Julia Pearlman to Stoughton PD; (2) documentation of that call; (3) the identity of the SPD employee who took the call and relayed it to Farwell; (4) records MSP provided to OCME. People Magazine requests all investigative reports including Trooper Dunne's 2021 reports. Fanning FOIA
Context: the Jan. 20, 2021 call
Per trial testimony (Fitton, Day 7): dispatcher James Fitton took the 7:40 PM call, informed Farwell in the detectives office, and Farwell became visibly and audibly angry. Officer Letendr also told Fitton to "forget about the call." Farwell then texted Fitton: "Let's never discuss that again. That girl is fucking insane." Chief McNamara first learned of this call in spring 2024 from the FBI. The recording was likely overwritten after 30 days. The identity of the employee who passed the information to Farwell, including Letendr's role, remains an open PRR question in the FOIA record at this date.
