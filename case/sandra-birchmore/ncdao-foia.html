Items of note in this production
09/23/2022
NDAO's public "no prosecutable case" statement
Traub's official statement to three outlets: "investigation to date has not developed a prosecutable statutory rape case against any individual." Sent to GBH, Boston 25, and the Globe in a single coordinated response.
02/10/2022
SPR21/3034 in-camera determination
After a closed inspection, Supervisor Rebecca Murray upholds NDAO's withholding. Stoughton IA investigation, still described as active 11 months after Sandra's death, remains the legal shield for all records.
05/12/2021
Fanning asks if tox covered "knockout" drugs
Fanning asked OCME if the standard panel would detect "roofies." OCME explained that a specific drug must be ordered separately. No record in this production that such a test was ever requested.
03/31/2021
Globe PRR denied the day it was filed
The first formal Crimaldi PRR was denied the same day it was filed. A denial chain running through SPR21/0846, SPR21/2185, SPR21/2518, and SPR21/3034, four SPR appeals, followed before records began releasing in 2022.
Act 1 · OCME Coordination, DNA, First Records Request / Feb – Aug 2021
02/04/2021
Fanning Briefs NDAO Leadership on the Case
Fanning briefs DA Morrissey, ADA Beland, and spokesman Traub. Characterization: "more investigative work needed." Sandra described in internal notes as a teacher from Sharon. NCDAO FOIA
02/09/2021
Fanning Contacts OCME: DNA Interest
Fanning contacts OCME to arrange a call with Dr. Capo Martinez regarding "some issues that came up." Specific interest in DNA. NCDAO FOIA

Sandra's Cousin Finds Letters and Notebooks: Then Hands Them to an Attorney
Dunne reports: Sandra's cousin found letters and notebooks at the Stoughton family home, initially offered to bring them to Dunne, then called back and gave them to an attorney instead. NCDAO FOIA
02/24/2021
Fanning's Final Request to OCME Before Body Released
Fanning tells OCME what they are looking for before Sandra's body is released to the family. Content and OCME's response both redacted in full. NCDAO FOIAFully redacted
03/2021
DNA Lab Coordination; MSP Can't Do Testing: Transfer to Bodie Labs
MSP Crime Lab forensic scientist Waite asks Dunne and Fanning what specific DNA testing they want "in light of the results I gave to Trooper Dunne yesterday." MSP cannot perform the requested testing; Fanning to facilitate transfer "likely to Bodie Labs." Case number assigned: 2021-112-0039. NCDAO FOIA
03/13/2021
OCME Photos: "Horrible Pics"
Internal exchange between Fanning and ADA Mattaliano regarding the scene photographs from OCME. Mattaliano: "At least they seem to be fine with DNA." NCDAO FOIA
03/16/2021
OCME Confirms Blood Tube Chain of Custody
OCME confirms grey-top blood tube exists with other items [redacted]; Fanning to coordinate lab transfer. Associated case reference: Sandra Birchmore / 2021-2032. NCDAO FOIAItems redacted
05/12/2021
Fanning Asks OCME Whether Tox Panel Covered "Knockout" Drugs
Fanning asks OCME whether the standard tox panel would detect drugs that could "knock someone out", specifically naming "roofies" (flunitrazepam/GHB). OCME (Kelly) explains: standard tox panel detects and quantifies a range of synthetic drugs, but a specific compound must be separately ordered and tested for individually. NCDAO FOIAFlagged
Open question: The standard tox report on Sandra Birchmore is back by this date. Fanning explicitly asked whether a GHB-type drug would appear. Kelly's answer was that it would not unless specifically ordered. No record in this production that a targeted GHB/flunitrazepam test was ever requested or run.
05/25/2021
Lab Results Forwarded
Lab results PDF forwarded Dunne to Fanning; Fanning confirms receipt. Attachment redacted in full in this production. NCDAO FOIARedacted
07/20 to 07/21/2021
Globe Asks for Update; Fanning Schedules NDAO Connect
Crimaldi asks Traub about the Birchmore report status. Traub: "Not time to submit yet." Separately, Fanning emails Beland, Morrissey, and Mattaliano to "connect on this case." NCDAO FOIA
08/06 to 08/20/2021
Globe Files Comprehensive PRR: Denied 14 Days Later
Globe (Crimaldi) files a formal PRR (2021-60) for all records concerning the Birchmore death investigation (08/06/2021). NDAO acknowledges receipt 08-09, citing a 10-business-day response window. Formal denial sent 08/20/2021: active investigation exemption (G.L. c. 4, §7(26)(f)). NCDAO FOIAExemption (f)
Note: This denial (and a prior one on 03/31/2021) triggered a four-appeal chain: SPR21/0846 → SPR21/2185 → SPR21/2518 → SPR21/3034. The Supervisor of Records upheld NDAO's withholding each time. Records did not begin releasing until after Farwell's resignation in March 2022.
Act 2 · Globe PRR Battle & SPR Appeals / Aug – Dec 2021
08/16/2021
Brady Trigger: "Officer Matthew G. Farwell"
Monahan emails Alford: subject line "Birchmore/Stoughton PD Investigation, Officer Matthew G. Farwell." This email initiates the Brady disclosure chain internally; Alford forwards to ADA Connolly 08/19/2021. NCDAO FOIA
08/26/2021
Globe Files First SPR Appeal: SPR21/2185
Globe appeals NDAO's 08/20/2021 denial to the Supervisor of Public Records. SPR21/2185 determination issued 09/10/2021. NCDAO FOIA
09/29/2021
Second Globe Appeal: SPR21/2518
Globe files a second SPR appeal. SPR21/2518 determination issued 10/14/2021. NCDAO FOIA
10/19/2021
Stoughton PD Counsel Confirms IA Still Active: Interviews Expected Dec. 2021
Stoughton PD's counsel confirms the IA investigation of Farwell remains active. Officer interviews anticipated in December 2021. NDAO uses this to justify continued withholding. NCDAO FOIA
11/17 to 11/18/2021
Third Globe Appeal: SPR21/3034
Globe files a third SPR appeal. NDAO forwards to Beland, Alford, and Fanning. NCDAO FOIA
12/20/2021 to 02/10/2022
Supervisor Orders In-Camera Review: Then Upholds NDAO Withholding
Supervisor Rebecca Murray orders an in-camera inspection of NDAO's withheld records (12/20/2021). In-camera conference call held 01/21/2022. Determination issued 02/10/2022: Supervisor upholds NDAO's Exemption (f) withholding in full. Stoughton PD IA investigation, described as still active, remains the justification. Monahan forwards to Beland, Alford, Fanning, and Traub. NCDAO FOIASPR21/3034
Context: Sandra Birchmore died 02/01/2021. This determination came 12+ months later. At this point the Stoughton PD IA investigation has not resulted in officer interviews, and Farwell has not yet resigned. The in-camera review itself is sealed, what the Supervisor saw is not in this production.
Act 3 · ALS Testing, Farwell Resignation, IA Report / Jan – Sep 2022
01/14/2022
MSP Crime Lab Tests Farwell's Workplace: NEGATIVE
MSP Crime Scene Services (Forensic Scientist Jennifer Preisig) conducts an ALS (Alternate Light Source) exam at 499 West Street, Stoughton, the Stoughton Animal Control building and Farwell's workplace. Directed to a specific room; surfaces tested: a couch and an office chair. ALS for [REDACTED substance]: NEGATIVE on both surfaces. No evidence collected or submitted. MSP lab: 22-01049; incident: 21-1-IA. NCDAO FOIASubstance redactedFlagged
Open question: The tested substance is fully redacted. This building is also where Joshua Heal worked, identified at trial (Kelsch, Day 6) as the biological father of Sandra's unborn baby. The exam occurs 11+ months after Sandra's death and one month before the SPR21/3034 in-camera determination.
03/11 to 04/05/2022
Farwell Resigns; NDAO Begins Releasing Records
Farwell resigns from Stoughton PD 03/11/2022. Crimaldi asks about Brady list status and additional record releases. NDAO coordinates with Stoughton PD on lifting the withholding objection. By 03-31: initial response to renewed PRR (2022-22) sent. By 04-05: supplemental response with additional reports. NCDAO FOIA
04/01/2022
Globe Asks Three Questions: NDAO Declines Comment
Crimaldi asks: (1) criminal charges? (2) statutory rape? (3) what did the investigation find about Farwell's relationship with Birchmore? NDAO (Traub): "The office declines comment." NCDAO FOIA
04/13/2022
Globe: "Scandal Widens in Stoughton Police Department"
Globe reports William Farwell (Matthew's father) and Robert Devine placed on paid administrative leave. Matthew's 2010 employment questionnaire listed Devine as his supervisor. Traub circulates article to all NDAO leadership; Morrissey forwards to personal contacts. NCDAO FOIA
05/06 to 05/16/2022
MSP Legal Coordinates Globe's Separate PRR With Tully and NDAO
MSP legal (Noone) contacts Monahan about Globe's PRR for MSP Birchmore records. D.Lt. Brian Tully (SPDU) loops in Monahan and Fanning to coordinate.
Note: Tully personally invited Farwell to the Oct. 2019 Norfolk DA MSP Homicide Conference (trial evidence, Ex. 296 / Beckstrom Day 5) and subsequently cleared Farwell of involvement in Sandra's death. He is now coordinating the Globe's records requests.
NCDAO FOIA
08/02/2022
William Farwell Resigns; Both Farwells and Devine on Brady List
William Farwell resigns. Globe confirms both William Farwell and Robert Devine are on the NDAO Brady/officer disclosure list. Traub to Crimaldi: "Yes, both are on officer disclosure list." NCDAO FOIA
09/22 to 09/23/2022
Stoughton Releases IA Report; NDAO Issues "No Prosecutable Case" Statement
Stoughton PD releases its 60-page IA report. Globe publishes: "Three Stoughton police officers had inappropriate relationships with a young woman." GBH News, Boston 25, and other outlets contact NDAO for comment. Traub issues a coordinated statement to all three: NCDAO FOIA
Full statement text
"After State Police detectives and the medical examiner found no evidence of foul play in the death of Ms. Birchmore, the Norfolk District Attorney's Office referred information developed in the death investigation to the Stoughton Police Department for action... investigation to date has not developed a prosecutable statutory rape case against any individual."

Traub to Crimaldi after her story ran: "Kudos for your story today."
Act 4 · Barstool, Comprehensive PRRs, Saylor Release / Sept – Dec 2022
09/27/2022
Barstool Sports PRR Names Joshua Heal: Three Years Before Patriot Ledger Story
Barstool Sports reporter Dave Cullinane files a PRR for records on Birchmore, Devine, Matthew Farwell, William Farwell, and Joshua Heal. First appearance of Heal's name in the NCDAO production. The same day Globe files its own PRR for William Farwell's MSP statement. NCDAO FOIAFlagged
Why it matters: Heal is identified at trial (Kelsch, Day 6) as the biological father of Sandra's unborn baby. The Patriot Ledger ran its "fourth ex-cop implicated" story on 07/27/2025, nearly three years after Cullinane named Heal in this PRR. Globe and other outlets' concurrent PRRs do not name Heal at this date.
09/28/2022
Globe Comprehensive PRR; Morrissey Texts Monahan
Globe files a comprehensive PRR for all remaining investigation materials. DA Morrissey texts ADA Monahan: "when you get a chance could you give me a call. No rush." NCDAO FOIA
10/12/2022
NDAO Responds to Globe and Cullinane PRRs; Serial #1 Text Disclosed
NDAO responds to Globe's 09-28 PRR (2022-81) and to Cullinane's 09-27 PRR. Globe response includes the full text of Dunne's Report Serial #1: no signs of struggle; Birchmore found on bedroom floor; cell phone 12 inches from right hand. NCDAO FOIA
10/20 to 10/25/2022
Marullo (Estate Attorney) Signals Records Request; Saylor PRR Released
Dunne notifies Beland and Fanning: Atty. Steve Marullo (representing the Birchmore estate) intends to file records requests. Separately, Laura Saylor (private requester who had previously met with Fanning) files a PRR; NDAO responds 11-08 with all 25 MSP serial reports (2021-112-39, Serials 1-25). Statutory rape references withheld under G.L. c. 41, §97D; Sandra's personal messages withheld as criminal investigation ongoing. NCDAO FOIA
11/08/2022
Cullinane Files Sweeping MSP Email PRR
Cullinane (Barstool) files a PRR for all MSP emails and memos mentioning "Birchmore," "Sandra Birchmore," or "Farwell" from Feb. 1, 2021 to present. NDAO forwards to leadership; ADA Brandon Hunt assigned to coordinate response on return. NCDAO FOIA
11/14 to 12/21/2022
NDAO Coordinates Marullo Records Release
Monahan reaches out to Marullo to confirm representation; requests written confirmation before proceeding with production. Records response (2022-100) sent to Marullo 12/21/2022 after leadership approval. NCDAO FOIA

03/23/2023
Marullo Formally Requests Autopsy Report for Next of Kin
Atty. Marullo submits a formal request under 505 CMR 1.03 for NDAO to provide a letter releasing Sandra Birchmore's autopsy report to next of kin. Hunt forwards to Beland. NCDAO FOIA
04/25/2023
Canton PD Asks NDAO About Release for Smith v. Farwell
Canton PD Deputy Chief Patty Sherrill contacts Hunt: Boyle/Shaughnessy Law filed a PRR representing William Farwell in Smith v. Farwell (2282CV01197), brought by Darlene Smith, Sandra's aunt. NCDAO FOIA
05/16/2023

Hunt sends formal response to Boyle/Shaughnessy PRR (2023-56): MSP Reports Serials 1-25 (2021-112-39), apartment surveillance video from 02/01/2021 (8 motion-triggered clips; DVR time stamp 13 minutes fast), and the 60-page Stoughton PD IA report. NCDAO FOIA

Nathaniel Story (statereference.com) files a PRR for all NDAO responses to Crimaldi's 2021 PRR, including the three SPR appeal packets (SPR21/2185, SPR21/2518, SPR21/3034). NDAO (Hunt) provides full response 07/12/2023. NCDAO FOIA
Late 2023
