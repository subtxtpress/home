PRE-TESTIMONY HOUSEKEEPING / DOCKET 361
  Docket 361 entered (Doc 361, filed 10/19/2026, Casper C.J.): Court ORDER requiring
    the government to remove and not post or distribute any trial exhibits before a
    verdict is rendered.
  Basis: On Friday (10/17/2026) the court was notified that the government had posted
    admitted trial exhibits -- including photos of Sandra Birchmore's body as discovered
    02/04/2021, items seized from the alleged crime scene, journal entries, and
    electronic device data -- on its publicly available website.
  Court found: jury must be free from outside influences; public interest in open
    judicial process is preserved through Courtroom 10, two overflow courtrooms with
    closed-circuit video feed, and continued media reporting.
  Authority: L.R. 83.2.1 (attorney release of information); L.R. 83.2.2 (special order
    in widely publicized/sensational trial); In re Globe Newspaper Co., 920 F.2d 88, 97
    n.10 (1st Cir. 1990); United States v. Chin, 913 F.3d 251, 258 (1st Cir. 2019);
    D. 295 (09/30/2026 trial order, ¶ 5).
  Effective through verdict or further order of the court.
  Defense exhibits E-11 / G-11: 412 concerns; defense to lay foundation through
    Kelsch; judge not convinced they fall under any 412 exception.
  Defense exhibits Q-10 / R-10: Dr. Leonard's psychiatric records (Leonard deceased);
    government objects (hearsay, not Sandra's treatment records); court to hear
    foundation and may require redactions.
  Exhibit 751 (Sandra's school records): attendance portion allowed; other pages
    reserved.
  Defense exhibit H-11 ("beyond pissed" letter): financial relevance; limited
    government objection.
  Affairs evidence (Docket 338): ruled ADMISSIBLE in limited form with 404(b)
    limiting instruction; parameters to be discussed end of day.
