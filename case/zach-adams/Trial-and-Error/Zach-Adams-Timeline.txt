JANUARY 2012 — DNA testing deferred
- During the search of the Britt property, a blonde hair is found in the Vandura van, in a rope with duct tape.

- The timing of the remains discovery coinciding with new leadership in the DA's office and sheriff's department and Stone's connections to people central to the pearl theft investigation raise questions about whether the discovery was truly accidental or whether the search priorities shifted with the new administration.

- Holly's skeletal remains recovered approx 20 miles from her home at Kelly Ridge, in a remote wooded area near (but not visible from) I-40.

HOW THE INTERROGATION PROCEEDS:
- Agents use a consistent pattern: introduce a fact Dylan has not stated; express it as confirmation of something Dylan said; then ask a question whose answer treats the injected fact as established.
- When Dylan pushes back, the agents simply repeat the false framing with more confidence until Dylan acquiesces.
- Dylan cannot recall things one would expect him to know, phone numbers for people he called regularly, for example, consistent with his documented memory deficits.
- Dylan describes Holly as wearing "long pants, pink shirt" (the description everyone publicly knew at the time). 
– He mentions no blood, no specific injuries consistent with the crime scene.
- Dylan blushes visibly when agents introduce sexual terms; Dr. Spirko: "A person who actually participated in a violent rape would not use polite, clinical, embarrassed language when describing it."
- When asked about blood at the scene, Dylan describes minor injuries (black eye, small cut on lip). 
– Agents note two pools of blood had formed on the floor; if Dylan's account were true, his DNA would be in that blood.
– No DNA evidence ever connects Dylan to any crime scene.
- Agents tell Dylan what he is supposed to remember rather than asking him to recall independently.
- The agents appear frustrated that Benjamin has not adequately prepped Dylan; they must supply details repeatedly that Dylan cannot produce. [T& E8; Dylan-Interrogation]

SEPTEMBER 2017 — SHELL CASING / .380 FORENSIC ERROR — CRITICAL
- A .380 auto shell casing was found right next to Holly Bobo's skull at the recovery site, the location where US Marshal Walker concluded she was killed, consistent with her cell phone data. [Undisclosed S9 E6]
- Forensic pathologist Marco Ross testified that the exit wound defect in Holly's skull measured .36 inches. He stated that any caliber bullet .36 inches or smaller could have caused the wound, and anything larger could not. [Undisclosed S9 E6]
- Prosecutor Jennifer Nichols used this in her rebuttal closing to argue that a .38 shell casing was excluded as the murder weapon because .38 > .36. 
- Defense counsel did not object. [Undisclosed S9 E6]
- THE ERROR: Nichols confused the shell casing diameter with the bullet diameter. 
- A .380 AUTO shell casing ejects when fired; only the bullet enters the skull. 
- The bullet diameter of a .380 auto round is .355 inches, not .38. 
- This is a closer match to the .36 inch exit wound defect than the .32 caliber (the state's claimed murder weapon). [Undisclosed S9 E6]
- At a later hearing, Zach's trial attorney acknowledged she did not understand the distinction between bullet diameter and shell casing diameter at the time of trial, and therefore did not object to Nichols's misstatement. 
- The .380 shell casing found right next to Holly's skull is now recognized as a viable, and arguably better-fitting, candidate for the murder weapon than the .32 pistol from the Dinsmore pond. [Undisclosed S9 E6]
- Bryant Vitt, neighbor of Shayne Austin's grandmother: Was outside mowing his lawn that morning, was a former police officer, and saw nothing suspicious next door. 
- Autry had testified that the rape of Holly occurred at Shayne's grandmother's house while Vitt was mowing.
- Vitt's testimony of seeing nothing contradicts this. [Undisclosed S9 E6]

CAST OF CHARACTERS

Holly Bobo — victim; 20-year-old nursing student from Darden, TN; abducted Apr 13, 2011; remains found Sept 1, 2014.
Clint Bobo — Holly's older brother; eyewitness to the abduction from the house; described the abductor. His DCS caseworker role (Tara Carrington was his client) is part of Benjamin's witness network.
Karen Bobo — Holly's mother.
Dana Bobo — Holly's father; first witness at the 2017 trial.
Terry Britt — convicted kidnapper and rapist; law enforcement's primary alternate suspect; Booth's January 2012 wiretap affidavit names him as having probable cause for Holly's kidnapping and murder; calls Walker's bluff in spring 2013 by essentially admitting guilt; a photo of Holly was found in his tool shed during the January 2012 search.
Janet Britt — Terry Britt's wife (seventh marriage); former husband Ed Martin convicted of raping foster minors; her alibi for Apr 13, 2011 is contradicted by her co-worker at the Parsons Newsletter.
Zach Adams — convicted 2017 of kidnapping, rape, murder; sentenced to life plus 50 years; the subject of post-conviction proceedings.
Dylan Adams — Zach's younger brother; documented frontal lobe deficits affecting source memory and false memory distinction; source of September 2014 false confession; pled guilty; 35 years. Placed in Benjamin's home as condition of federal probation.
Shayne Austin — fourth co-defendant; passed two polygraphs; no physical evidence against him; died by suicide in Bartow, Florida, February 2015; immunity was revoked before his death.
Jason Autry — co-defendant who became state's star witness at Zach's trial; recanted entire testimony in December 2023 interview with Dr. Spirko.
Victor Dinsmore — convicted rapist turned trial witness; drug dealer (morphine) in Decatur County; confronted by Dennis Benjamin in spring 2014 and coached by TBI SA Brent Booth in May 2017; testified about alleged murder weapon (a gun traded to him by Shayne Austin) and alleged truck hidden in his pole barn; testimony shown to be false and coached. Holly's personal effects found on the road east and west of his property.
Dennis Benjamin — retired Memphis police officer / "the COP" (Chief of Port); inserted himself into the investigation; coached witnesses including Dinsmore and Dylan Adams; in a romantic relationship with Beth Boswell; connected to TBI through Jack Vanhuser, Jeff Jackson, and Valerie Trout; had Dylan living in his Memphis home for ~6 weeks before the September 2014 interrogation.
Brent Booth — TBI Special Agent; signed the January 2012 wiretap affidavit naming Britt as the likely perpetrator; later coached Dinsmore on testimony in May 2017; also interrogated Dylan Adams in September 2014.
Jeff Jackson — TBI agent; led the September 17, 2014 Dylan Adams interrogation; met with Benjamin before the interrogation to get the full "confession" story.
Valerie Trout — TBI agent; handled witnesses whose stories could be shaped through DCS pressure; received Dinsmore's March 5, 2014 statement.
Jack Vanhuser — TBI Regional Special Agent in Charge; Dennis Benjamin's longtime buddy; Benjamin invoked his name in the recorded Dinsmore confrontation.
Jennifer Nichols — lead prosecutor; trained Autry as a witness per his recant; simultaneously prosecuted Britt plea, Autry plea, and others; connected to Benjamin through long Memphis-era overlap.
Beth Boswell — simultaneously Dylan Adams's court-appointed defense attorney, a federal and state prosecutor (handled Britt plea, Autry plea, and others), and in a romantic relationship with Dennis Benjamin. Dylan's sealed federal docket (25 missing documents, sealed entries) was under her handling.
Terry Dicus — TBI lead agent; later retired; testified for the defense at Zach's trial that evidence pointed to Britt, not Adams.
Art Viveros — FBI Special Agent; prosecuted Britt on the Tequila Jones case; testified for the defense at the 2017 trial.
John Walker — retired Senior Inspector, U.S. Marshals; reconstructed Holly's cell phone route; confronted Britt in spring 2013; testified for the defense; continues to investigate.
Dana McLendon — criminal defense attorney; Zach Adams's current post-conviction counsel; co-host of Trial and Error podcast.
Dr. Katie Spirko — neuropsychologist, court-appointed expert witness; conducted the December 2023 Autry recant interview; analyzed Dylan Adams's frontal lobe deficits in depth; co-host of Trial and Error podcast. Under state subpoena as of Ep. 9 (July 2025), prevented from participating.
Michael Scholl — Autry's defense attorney; per Autry's recant, coordinated story development with the prosecution.
Clark Chapman — private investigator hired by Scholl; per Autry's recant, helped develop the fabricated story.
Judge Creed McGinley — approved the January 2012 Britt wiretap; presided over the 2017 Zach Adams trial.
Hansel McAdams — lead prosecutor who signed off on the Booth wiretap affidavit naming Britt.
Mr. Barnes — neighbor of the Bobo family; heard Holly screaming during the abduction on April 13, 2011; encountered again by Walker and McLendon at the Bobo home on January 18, 2025.
Ricky Inman — Decatur County Sheriff's deputy; received Dinsmore's Jul 21, 2011 statement; also present at the ~2003 investigation of Janet Martin's rented West 9th Street residence.
Carl Statler — testified at trial to incriminating statements allegedly made by Zach Adams; dating Amber Dinsmore (Victor's daughter); part of Benjamin's witness network.
Jaime Darnell — testified against Zach at trial; married to Carl Statler's sister Danielle; appears in the Dinsmore coaching sessions in connection with the alleged murder weapon.
Brian Vit — Victor Dinsmore's neighbor; off-duty cop; late-filed state witness; part of Benjamin's witness network.
Tara Carrington — Jason Hugley's ex-girlfriend; DCS client whose caseworker was Clint Bobo; her statements led to Zach's first (later dropped) arrest; part of Benjamin's witness network.
Jason Kilzer — connected to the case; killed by law enforcement on Aug 8, 2025 while serving a failure to appear warrant, the week before Zach's last hearings; was unarmed; had expressed fear of law enforcement related to Zach's case; Dr. Spirko was with him hours before he was shot.
Sheila Kilzer — Jason Kilzer's mother; source for Episode 15; filed federal civil rights complaint Jul 31, 2026.
Cindy Adams — mother of Zach and Dylan Adams; source for Episode 12.
Natalie Bobo — Holly's cousin; encountered Terry Britt at Dollar General (February 2011) and at a house party before Apr 13, 2011.
Michael Alexander — implicated by Dinsmore in December 2011 as part of burial of Holly's body; claim later abandoned.
