SOURCE 1: DYLAN ADAMS — FULL TBI INTERROGATION
09-17-2014 (September 17, 2014)
Transcript: 2026-01-24-Coerced-Confession-of-Dylan-Adams-Full-Unedited-TBI-Interrogation-(Holly-Bobo-Case).md
Duration: 5:58 PM – approximately 10:15 PM (4+ hours)
Interviewer: TBI Agent Jackson (primary); at least one additional agent present
========================================

NOTE ON FORMAT: Timestamps below are from the interrogation recording, not real-world clock times.
The interrogation began at approximately 5:58 PM on 09-17-2014.

--- PRE-INTERROGATION COORDINATION ---

[09-17-2014] Before the interrogation opens, Jackson references having "got the gist of it" from Dennis
(Benjamin) prior to this session — confirming a pre-interrogation meeting between TBI and Dennis
Benjamin, the CI/jailhouse contact who brokered Dylan's cooperation, in which the basic outline of
what Dylan would say was established before the formal interview began.


--- EVENTS FROM DYLAN'S REAL INDEPENDENT ACCOUNT (what he said before agents built the story) ---

[04-13-2011 evening] Dylan's unprompted account: Jason Autry came over to Zach's house around 7-8 PM
the night before Holly disappeared. The group shot meth (not smoked — Dylan corrects agents on this
multiple times). Zach and Autry left together around midnight to 1 AM. Dylan says he was still at the
house when they left and did not see them again until 9:30-10 AM the following morning (which would
be the morning of April 13, 2011 — the day Holly disappeared). Dylan does not independently describe
any planning meeting, any mention of Holly Bobo, or Shane Austin. This is the account he gives before
agent framing begins.

[04-13-2011] Dylan says he did not go with Zach and Autry when they left at midnight/1 AM. He
remained at Zach's house. When they returned (by his account ~9:30-10 AM), Shane Austin was no
longer with them. Dylan says he doesn't know where Shane was at that point.


--- AGENT-LED FRAMING (how the coerced narrative is constructed) ---

[09-17-2014, early in interview — approx 0:00-0:30 on recording] Jackson immediately introduces the
rape framing within the first minutes of the interrogation: "discussion comes up about going and
getting someone to rape them." Dylan's response is to repeat the frame back ("They talking about
going get somebody. I mean, talking about finding someone to rape") — following the frame, not
generating the narrative independently.

[09-17-2014, approx 1:30-1:35 on recording] Agents introduce Shane Austin into the narrative.
Dylan had not mentioned Shane in his initial account. Agents ask whether Shane was at the planning
meeting, what Shane said, and how Shane "knew" Holly. Dylan begins to agree that Shane was present
and says Shane was dropped off by his parents around 7 PM — an account he himself describes as
"just assuming that."

[09-17-2014] Agents show Dylan a floor plan diagram of Zach's house. They ask him to mark where
the couch was, where the blood was, and which bedroom Holly was taken to. Dylan makes marks on
the diagram. Agents guide his hand/placement throughout — "make a mark right there," "this edge of
the couch," etc. Dylan signs and dates the diagram 9/17.

[09-17-2014] Agents introduce the description of Holly's injuries: "black eye, bloody nose, cut lip."
Dylan had not independently described any injuries to Holly. He agrees to these details after agents
introduce them.

[09-17-2014] Agents tell Dylan there is phone communication between him and Shane Austin "right
before, right during, right after the rape." Jackson says: "I know it." Dylan is then asked to explain
the communication. Dylan subsequently agrees he texted Shane Austin to say they were "on the way"
— a statement produced under direct pressure after agents assert the fact of phone contact.

[09-17-2014] Agents discuss a possible video of the assault. Jackson asks about a video Zach
supposedly made. Dylan says "I don't know of no video" initially but equivocates when agents push.
No video is ultimately confirmed in Dylan's account.

[09-17-2014, approx 3:01 on recording] Agents raise Victor Dinsmore's name. They ask Dylan why he
"put a man's name in this that wasn't involved." This is significant: in an earlier version of his
statement (prior to this recorded session), Dylan had apparently mentioned Dinsmore. Agents are now
asking him to clarify or walk back that mention. This is the only direct reference in this interrogation
to Dinsmore's connection to the case being known or mentioned by Dylan at this stage.


--- WHAT DYLAN CLAIMS TO KNOW (produced under agent pressure during interrogation) ---

[04-13-2011 evening, produced mid-interrogation] Dylan says the plan to take Holly was hatched that
same night at Zach's house while they were smoking meth. Shane Austin named Holly by name, said
he knew where she lived, described her as good-looking. The idea originated with Shane, not Zach.
Dylan says he agreed Holly was attractive. Dylan says Zach and Autry ("Train" — Autry's nickname)
then left with Holly in Dylan's truck; Shane Austin went with them initially, then was dropped off
at his grandmother's house before they returned to Zach's.

[04-13-2011 to 04-14-2011] Dylan says Zach, Jason ("Train"/Autry), and Shane Adams [sic — Austin]
left in Dylan's truck to get Holly. They returned to Zach's house between 9:30-10 AM with Holly.
Dylan says all three of them (Zach, Jason, and Dylan) raped Holly at Zach's house, bent over the
end of a green leather couch. Dylan says she was bleeding on the floor. The rape narrative detail —
including anal and vaginal rape, Holly's clothing (pinkish bra, matching underwear), the specific
couch positioning — is produced through sustained agent framing and direct questioning over multiple
hours, not volunteered by Dylan independently.

[04-14-2011] The day after (Thursday), Dylan says he, Zach, and Shane Austin drove to get Xanax
bars together. During this trip, Shane said the rape "was good." Zach's reaction is described as
"keep his mouth shut, that was good."

[04-14-2011] Dylan says after the rape he disposed of his clothes (which had blood on them) by
bagging them and putting them in a roadside trash dump the same day or the next morning. He also
says he changed his phone — he had his phone in his grandmother's name, later transferred to his
grandfather's name. He frames the phone transfer as a possible attempt to avoid phone evidence
linking him to the case.

[approx. 05-14-2011 or 06-2011 — "a month or two after"] Dylan says he overheard a phone
conversation between Zach and Autry ("Train") in which they were "freaking out" about a gun found
near Autry's house. The gun had apparently been found by police. Zach was asking Autry if they had
"found the gun below his house." This information is produced under heavy agent pressure after agents
assert Dylan already told this to other people.

[date uncertain, some time after April 2011] Dylan says Zach told him "I shot her" at some point
after the events — but Dylan walks this back during the interrogation, saying he "heard Zach on
the phone" saying it, and then shifts again. The claim is never cleanly established. The agent
presses: "Did you hear Zach say he had shot [her]?" Dylan confirms hearing Zach on a phone asking
about the gun "below his house," implying Zach had the gun.

[date uncertain] Dylan says he told his friend John Tubbs (John Dove?) that Holly was "shot in the
back of the head" and was "between the two sawmills." Agents reference John Tubbs as having reported
this to TBI and as having identified Dylan as the source. Dylan attributes his knowledge of this to
having "heard Jack (Zach?) saying" it. At one point Dylan says Autry shot Holly, then changes to
Zach, then claims he doesn't know. The agents press him to settle on Zach as the shooter.

[date uncertain] Zach Adams threatened Dylan: agents read the quote to Dylan: "You tell my brother
he's the one that started this shit and if he don't shut his mouth he'll be in the hole right beside
her." Dylan confirms he received this threat from Zach after Dylan talked to someone about the case.
Dylan says he was beaten severely by Zach — "took a long time," visible facial scars — and went to
hide at a friend's house ("Cub's house") for days afterward. This beating happened after Dylan told
John (Tubbs) something about the case that got back to Zach.


--- SHANE AUSTIN'S GRANDMOTHER'S HOUSE (produced near end of interrogation) ---

[04-13-2011 to 04-14-2011] Dylan claims Holly was held at Shane Austin's grandmother's house
(Wonita Hickerson) on Yellow Springs Road in Decatur County before being brought to Zach's house.
Dylan says he knows this because Shane "told me he had her in a basement." The house is described
as off "Pew Road," down a hill past a cemetery, with fields around it. Dylan says he and Shane once
got a truck out of that property. During the interrogation, agents pull up aerial imagery and Dylan
identifies the house. Dylan admits he was never inside the house himself. The claim that Holly was
held there before being brought to Zach's is based entirely on Shane's statement to Dylan — not
direct observation.


--- END-OF-INTERROGATION STRUCTURE ---

[09-17-2014, approx 3:02-3:25 on recording] Jackson calls a break. When they return, Jackson
tells Dylan: "Their opinion is the same as mine. I got problems with what you're saying. Period."
Jackson then tells Dylan he is going to jail that night and that the DA is waiting to hear what
was said. Jackson tells Dylan he will only be used as a witness if he is "man enough" to fully
cooperate. This is the moment at which the coercive deal structure is made explicit.

[09-17-2014, approx 3:31-4:00 on recording] Jackson summarizes Dylan's account for the record,
using inclusive "we" language and pressing Dylan to stop saying "they did" and start saying "we."
Jackson tells Dylan: "You are as involved as anybody." This is the agent explicitly framing Dylan
as a participant and training him to present himself that way in future court testimony.

[09-17-2014, approximately 10:14-10:15 PM] Jackson ends the interrogation on the record: "Time is
approximately 10:14 p.m. on 17th day of September, 2014. This concludes this interview."

[09-17-2014] Final exchange: a second agent asks if the basement story is confirmed — did Shane
specifically tell Dylan that was the house Holly was kept in? Dylan confirms: "He told me that the
basement." The interview ends at approximately 10:15 PM.

NOTE: The DA present for the end of this interrogation is not named in the transcript. This is
significant context given later revelations about Jennifer Nichols's role.


--- WITNESSES DYLAN SAYS HE TOLD ABOUT THE CASE ---

John Tubbs (John Dove?) — told him Holly was shot in the back of the head, was between the two
  sawmills. John Tubbs then told TBI.
Wesley Bird — told him something (details not specified in transcript)
Jason (unnamed last name) — told him something
WC Austin (Phillip Austin's son) — told him Zach shot Holly; this got back to Zach

SOURCE 2: JASON AUTRY — FULL JAILHOUSE INTERVIEW (RECANT)
04-25-2025 (April 25, 2025)
Transcript: 2025-04-25-Jason-Autrys-Full-Jailhouse-Interview-Recants-Says-Prosecutor-turned-Judge-Was-"Boss-of-it-All".md
Interviewer: Journalist/investigator (unnamed in transcript)
========================================


--- AUTRY'S WHEREABOUTS ON 04-13-2011 ---

[04-13-2011] Autry says he cannot independently recall what he did on April 13, 2011 (the day
Holly disappeared) except through phone records. "I'd have to agree to all that. Yeah. By those
phone records, I knew where I was that day." He heard about Holly's abduction that day, sometime
after noon, at Double D's bar ("it was after 12:00"). He was drinking. Someone told him to stay
away from the search area. Autry says he thinks he may have been at the river area that morning
with Zach ("I think me and Zach was actually down there at the river that morning... getting
high"), which is what his phone records reflected — placing him in that general area, though not
at the Bobo residence or at the crime scene.


--- AUTRY'S RELATIONSHIP TO HOLLY BOBO ---

[background] Holly Bobo was Autry's second cousin — connected through his grandmother's half
brother. "It wasn't no blood connection." Autry says he never sat at the family table with the
Bobos, never did holidays together. He describes it as a distant family connection he barely
maintained.

[shortly after 04-13-2011] Holly's father Dana Bobo came to Autry's house early in the investigation
("early in the morning") to talk and ask questions. Autry says he answered every question Dana had.
Dana was also driving to Shane Austin's house looking around. Autry says Dana was pulling up at
Shane's house, "looking around, and there he is in there with all them guns."


--- AUTRY'S ARREST AND LEGAL SITUATION ---

[background, approx 2012] Autry was already incarcerated in the federal system on a firearm charge
when he was arrested for the Holly Bobo case. He had been in federal custody "about a year" before
something Dylan said during his own sentencing put Autry's name in the case.

[approx 2012-2013] First defense team: Fletcher Long and others. They were pulled off the case
after getting in trouble. Mike (Flanigan) was then appointed. Flanigan was removed at the
defendant's (Autry's) attorney's request. Autry's attorney (Mike) brought his own team, which
included Clark Chapman.

[approx 2013-2014] Autry's attorney told him: "This is like a child molester case. Everybody's
going to scream poor baby. I got you about 95% chance you spend the rest of your time right over
there." This is the moment, per Autry, when the seed was planted that he might need to cooperate
with prosecutors.


--- HOW THE FALSE STORY WAS BUILT ---

[approx 2014-2016, over multiple attorney visits] Autry describes the story as constructed
collaboratively with his attorney and prosecutors over multiple sessions — not discovered or
confessed, but built. The process:

1. Attorney visits Autry in prison. The initial offer: testify about what he saw. Autry asks what
   they want. Attorney says: "I need you to testify" and implies Autry will go free in exchange.

2. Autry rehearses a version of the story with his attorney: "He said, 'Can you rehearse that
   story for today.'" When done, the attorney said, "That'll work."

3. The attorney takes the story to prosecutors to vet. He returns months later with adjustments.
   The pattern repeats over what Autry describes as 3-4-5-6 months between sessions.

4. Autry and attorney go to the actual river location together the day before he walks prosecutors
   through it: "We're going down the river tomorrow."

5. The story was assembled in approximately 3 days once they had full access to case materials
   (phone records, investigative reports, IR statements). "We put the story together in about
   3 days. And, you know, you get a story out, you do swear to it. Man, it's amazingly simple."

6. Autry says the case materials were provided by his attorney: "They just gave them to you like
   you had them yourself." These included phone records and investigative reports ("IR statements").
   "There's a lot of stuff in there... a whole box of just psychics [sic]."

7. Source of Holly's cause of death detail: TBI agents announced the skull and bullet hole
   discovery (a .38 caliber, Autry says) at Double D's bar when the skull was found. The
   information reached Autry through his mother. "Brent Booth told it to [his mother]." This
   is how Autry knew Holly was shot.

8. Source for placing Shane, Dylan, and Zach together: a logger report in the case files. Autry
   says a logger report described seeing three men together in the woods that morning, one of
   whom appeared to have been struggling. "That's where I got the idea that they were down there
   close." He explicitly says he does not know if they actually did it.

[ongoing, through trial preparation] Jennifer Nichols (then-prosecutor, later judge) is identified
by Autry as the central figure in story development: "Jennifer Nichols. Yeah. She was the boss of
it all." Nichols is identified as conducting witness preparation and vetting the story. Clark
Chapman (attorney) and Mike (Flanigan?) helped develop the narrative. Robert (another attorney)
was present but did little.

[trial preparation] Nichols coordinated the vetting process: "Jennifer Nichols said, 'We're going
to vet this story and it's got to be untouchable.'" She coached Autry's courtroom testimony: "I was
told who to speak to and how much to shut down, when to ask for more or less." Autry was trained
as a witness: short answers, look at the jury.

[trial preparation — multiple attorneys in room] At one point, four people questioned Autry
simultaneously — two attorneys and two prosecutors — at a rapid-fire pace to prepare him. Autry
describes this as the final polish stage before trial.


--- DETAILS AUTRY GOT WRONG (revealing the story was fabricated) ---

[trial testimony error] The fence and gate at Shane's property: Autry testified about a fence and
gate at Shane Austin's place as it looked on the day of the crime. The interviewer tells Autry the
fence didn't go up until a few months after Holly's disappearance — when Shane got a puppy. Autry
acknowledges this was wrong. He says if he were Shane's attorney, he would have made more of the
point that Autry "never saw nobody or shot nobody."

[gun testimony] Autry testified about a gun connected to Shane — a .357 with a spark (muzzle
brake?) on the end, described as a long barrel, black. Shane's father had shown Autry this gun.
Autry told Dinsmore this gun belonged to Shane, and Dinsmore either told police where it was or
"found" it. But the gun police actually found does not match Shane's .357 — the found gun was a
.32 or .38. Autry acknowledges these are different guns: "The gun they found and that gun's not
the same gun." Jimmy Austin (Shane's father) still has the .357, Autry believes.


--- AUTRY'S ACCOUNT OF ZACH ADAMS ---

[2012, Oine Federal Penitentiary] Autry and Zach Adams were incarcerated at the same facility —
Oine Federal Penitentiary — in 2012 at the same time. Both were facing gun charges. Autry says
Zach was a sex offender at that point (a prior charge) and Autry had gang affiliations (which he
says he left in 2009). They talked extensively.

[ongoing — multiple TBI reports] Zach's statement to TBI was consistent across 5-7 reports: he was
with Shane Austin and Dylan getting Xanax bars. Autry says the interviewer has the bank statements
corroborating this alibi. Zach also went to Joe's Video (Rebecca Herp working there), was heavily
intoxicated on Xanax, headbutted her, and someone called 911 — but no one responded because all
law enforcement was looking for Holly Bobo. Autry says that 911 call log should still be accessible
and would corroborate Zach's alibi timeline.

[alibi corroboration — ATM footage] The ATM security guard at the bank where Shane used the ATM
offered TBI video footage of the transaction to corroborate the exact time and date. Per Autry
(relaying what the interviewer told him): TBI declined to take the footage. The bank statement
exists; the video footage was available but TBI did not want it.


--- AUTRY ON TERRY BRITT ---

[2012, Oine Federal Penitentiary] While incarcerated at Oine with Zach in 2012, Autry encountered
Terry Britt. Britt was also on a gun charge — Britt said he was charged with a gun he didn't have,
and that the actual gun was a .38 special that he threw in "Black Bottom" (a location off the side
of Red Bud Lake in Lexington, Tennessee). Autry says this could have been the gun used in Holly
Bobo's death.

[ongoing, date uncertain] Britt's wife told Autry that Britt was "talking to people that weren't
there." Britt himself told Autry he saw a ghost in his kitchen — a young girl who would rattle the
pots and pans, then vanish when he approached. Autry's interpretation: the ghost is Holly Bobo.

[04-25-2025] Autry says he now believes Terry Britt committed the crime: "Looking at the reports.
Once the cadaver dog get on it, seeing all the tools that he had in the back of the van. Yeah,
I'm pretty confident." He wishes Britt had confessed in a jail cell to make things easier. Britt
never directly confessed to Autry — Autry's belief is based on the physical evidence profile and
the ghost story.


--- AUTRY'S STATE OF MIND / AFTERMATH OF TRIAL TESTIMONY ---

[09-2017 — after trial] After testifying at Zach Adams's trial, Autry attempted suicide multiple
times. He was placed in the suicide tank two or three times. In one instance, guards cut him down
from a hanging attempt. "They come in and cut me down one time." He describes testifying as "the
worst I've ever felt in my life coming back, knowing that I just lied about an innocent man [and]
put [him] in prison."

[post-trial, multiple times] Prison inmates ridiculed Autry daily: "You killed her, you no good
rap and killing." He describes this as a persistent source of psychological torment that "drove some
of my anger" and caused him to continue telling a version of the false story even after testifying
("I guess I acted out with that false story and let them plant the seeds like that").

[12-2023 / early 2025] Autry's attorney appeared via Zoom a few days before Autry's prison release.
He told Autry: "Be careful when you're around these gas stations and bars and stuff like that.
Anybody I will pull that old guy and shoot you right." Autry took this as a threat connected to his
knowledge of the false testimony. When released, he immediately received a gun from someone a mile
down the road, which he eventually traded for a deer rifle at "Skies." This is the firearms charge
that re-incarcerated him.

[04-25-2025] Autry commits to helping secure Zach Adams's release: "I want to see his release. I
am dedicated to that... it's the right thing to do." He is considering going to the media. He says
he plans to visit Zach Adams at Thompson Federal Penitentiary where Zach is currently held. Autry
does not know if Zach will talk to him.


--- AUTRY ON THE PROSECUTION / JENNIFER NICHOLS ---

[trial preparation and trial] Jennifer Nichols is described as the architect of the prosecution
narrative: "She was the boss of it all." Autry says she "vetted" his story, trained him as a
witness, and coordinated with Clark Chapman and his attorney to refine the testimony. Autry says:
"I think she care less long as there ain't nobody on TV other than that [the case being closed].
I bet three hours after that trial was over, Jennifer Nichols... I bet she ain't spent much time on
it." Autry does not believe Nichols personally cared whether the right person was convicted.

[04-25-2025] Autry believes Karen and Dana Bobo (Holly's parents) were complicit in directing who
was prosecuted: "I believe those three boys is who Karen and Dana wanted arrested. I believe they
was prosecuted under their permission and I was let go by their permission." He says the judge
(Jennifer Nichols by this point?) asked the Bobos at sentencing whether they were satisfied. They
said yes. Autry's attorney told him: "Somebody's got to pay, Jason. Did he gonna be you? Hell no."


--- AUTRY ON WHAT HE ACTUALLY KNOWS ---

[04-25-2025] Autry explicitly says he went back and forth for 18 months — from the time he got
statements and reports — wondering if Shane Austin and the others actually did commit the crime.
"I went through a phase of thinking that. I don't anymore." He says after reviewing the full
record, he no longer believes Zach, Dylan, or Shane are guilty.

[04-25-2025] On Shane Austin specifically: Autry says he "never really liked Shane too much" and
believed Shane was "capable of something like that" due to "lack of attention growing up." But he
says after reviewing the evidence, he does not believe Shane, Dylan, or Zach are guilty. On Zach:
"No, not really [did I get that sense from him]."

[04-25-2025] The only thing Autry says he is "sure" of is that the story he told at trial was
fabricated collaboratively with prosecutors and his attorney, that Jennifer Nichols supervised and
vetted the story, and that Zach Adams is innocent.
