TERRY BRITT — CRIMINAL HISTORY (pre-2011)
==========================================================================

JUNE 1977
- Terry Britt's first arrest: rape of a 16-year-old blonde girl taken into
  the woods.

1978
- Britt convicted of rape. Sentenced to 10 years.

1977–2008 (cumulative)
- Additional Tennessee arrests: robbery, DUI (multiple), public intoxication
  (multiple), arson, sexual battery, burglary, receiving/concealing stolen
  property, assault and battery, theft, aggravated rape, kidnapping
  (multiple), phone harassment.
- Additional charges in North Carolina not reflected in Tennessee record.
- Multiple victims who did not pursue charges through law enforcement.

1995
- Britt convicted of aggravated rape and kidnapping. Sentenced to 8 years.

~2000
- Photographs show Britt with shoulder-length or longer hair kept in a
  ponytail. [Ep. 4]

~2003
- Decatur County Sheriff's deputy Ricky Inman and Parsons PD Officer Scott
  Cagle respond to a possible break-in at a residence on West 9th Street,
  Parsons, secretly rented by Janet Martin (later Janet Britt).
- The residence has a room with cameras on stands, a small cot, wired to a
  computer in the adjacent room.
- Janet declines to file a report and asks that no one be told she is renting
  it. [Ep. 4]

~2004
- Janet Martin meets Terry Britt while visiting her ex-husband Ed Martin in
  prison (Ed Martin convicted of raping minors in their foster care; Janet
  had reportedly known about the abuse and alerted him when authorities were
  closing in).
- Janet and Terry begin a relationship; she eventually becomes his seventh
  wife. [Ep. 4]

2007–2008
- John Walker, while off-duty at the Tennessee River near I-40 Bridge, finds
  a decomposed body floating — kept partially afloat by intestinal gases.
  He calls police. This real incident is later incorporated by Jason Autry
  into his fabricated trial testimony as the disposal plan for Holly's body.
  [Ep. 2 / Ep. 3 Pt. 1 — RECANT]

2008
- Charges filed against Terry and Janet Britt for kidnapping and sexual
  assault of a woman named Tequila Jones. Janet was in the vehicle during
  the attack. Charges initially dismissed; later picked up and prosecuted
  by FBI Special Agent Art Viveros. Britt pleads out.
- Note: If prosecuted in 2008 as originally charged, Britt would have been
  in prison when Holly Bobo was abducted in April 2011. [Ep. 4]

APRIL 13, 2011 — MORNING OF HOLLY BOBO'S ABDUCTION
==========================================================================

PRE-DAWN / EARLY MORNING
- Drew Scott, a neighbor, is active in the area. His phone data places him
  at home during the relevant window. [Ep. 13]

BEFORE 7:45 A.M.
- Dana Bobo (father) leaves for work. Karen Bobo (mother) leaves for work
  separately. Holly is at home with her older brother Clint.
- Clint's car is in the closed garage in back — he had washed it and didn't
  want it to get dirty. It would not be visible to anyone watching the
  property. An outside observer would assume Holly was home alone. [Ep. 1]

BEFORE 7:45 A.M.
- Walker's theory: the abductor had come up through the woods via an old
  logging road behind the house, watched, and waited until both parents
  left. [Ep. 1 — WALKER]

~7:45 A.M.
- Neighbor Mr. Barnes steps outside to put oil in his pickup truck at his
  property approximately 100 yards from the Bobo house. The field between
  the properties was clear of trees at the time — direct sightline. [Ep. 1]

~7:45 A.M.
- Man comes out of the woods. Holly is in or near the carport. Barnes hears
  Holly screaming for approximately 45 seconds: "Stop it, don't, stop it."
  Barnes says it sounded like she knew the man but did not know his name.
  Screaming stops abruptly. [Ep. 1]

~7:45 A.M.
- Barnes shuts his truck hood without replacing the oil cap. Gets his
  girlfriend and child. Drives toward the Bobo house. [Ep. 1]

7:46 A.M.
- Barnes is at the bottom of the Bobo driveway, looking up it. He does not
  see anything happening. He calls his mother to report what he heard. [Ep. 1]

7:46 A.M. (simultaneous)
- Clint Bobo looks out from the house and sees Holly squatting down,
  face-to-face with a man, talking quietly. He assumes it is Holly and her
  boyfriend having an argument and doesn't want to get involved. He tries
  to call his mother. No answer. [Ep. 1]

~7:50 A.M.
- Clint tries his mother again. No answer. [Ep. 1]

~7:50–7:56 A.M.
- Barnes calls his mother to report what he heard. [Ep. 1]

7:56 A.M.
- Clint reaches his mother Karen. While talking to her on the phone, he
  sees Holly and the man walking across the backyard toward the woods. The
  man has his left arm around Holly. Holly has her possessions with her.
  [Ep. 1]
- Karen tells Clint something is wrong — get the gun and go after your
  sister. Holly's boyfriend was many miles away turkey hunting; Karen knew
  it could not be him. [Ep. 1]

APPROXIMATELY 7:56–8:00 A.M.
- Holly is walked from the backyard through the woods, most likely to Swan
  Johnson Road, where Walker theorizes the abductor's vehicle was parked
  just past the residence. [Ep. 1 — WALKER]

~8:00 A.M.
- Holly's phone begins moving north from the Bobo home. [Ep. 1 — WALKER;
  Ep. 13]

~8:15 A.M.
- A chief deputy arrives at the Bobo home. He checks the logging road and
  observes a standing pool of water covering the full width of the road that
  has not been disturbed — ruling out that the abductor used that road for
  a vehicle. [Ep. 1]

~8:15 A.M. ONWARD
- Dozens of law enforcement officers from multiple agencies converge on the
  Bobo home almost immediately. Hundreds of community members also arrive.
  Helicopters are deployed. Mass search crews mobilize. [Ep. 1; Ep. 3 Pt. 1]

8:39 A.M.
- Facebook records confirm Zach Adams, Dylan Adams, and Shane Austin are
  together and active on social media at this time, corroborating their
  alibi. This is inconsistent with any scenario placing them at the Bobo
  home at the time of abduction. [Ep. 10]

~8:39–9:30 A.M.
- Holly's phone travels north approximately 20 miles on back roads and sits
  stationary for approximately 25–30 minutes at the site near Kelly Ridge
  where her remains will be found three and a half years later.
- Walker's conclusion: this is when and where Holly was killed.
- FBI profiler dispatched from Washington, D.C. in the first weeks of
  investigation had said Holly was killed in the first hour within five miles
  of the house by probably a single individual. This assessment proves
  accurate. [Ep. 1 — WALKER]

~9:30 A.M.
- Holly's phone begins moving south from the Kelly Ridge area. It travels
  back along the same route.
- Along this route, Holly's notebook, lunchbox, and other personal
  belongings are later found discarded. [Ep. 1 — WALKER]

~9:30–10:00 A.M.
- Holly's phone goes dark approximately 4 miles (approximately 8 minutes of
  driving) from Terry Britt's trailer on Jeanette Holiday Road, Parsons.
- If proceeding directly from the phone's last location to Britt's property,
  this is the direct route. [Ep. 1 — WALKER; Ep. 13]

~10:00 A.M.
- Janet Britt is at work at the Parsons Newsletter, the only local newspaper
  in town. [Ep. 4]

~10:30 A.M.
- Terry Britt calls Janet at work and tells her she needs to leave. She
  calls back. Terry shows up at the Newsletter in one of their vans — not
  the Ford F-150 he will later claim she drove all day — and picks her up.
  A co-worker witnesses the call and the pickup. [Ep. 4]
- During the morning, Terry and Janet Britt's separate cell phones are in
  contact while bouncing off different cell towers, indicating they were in
  different locations — directly contradicting Britt's claim they were
  together all day. [Ep. 4]

11:12 A.M.
- ATM security footage at a local bank captures the truck belonging to Zach
  Adams, Dylan Adams, and Shane Austin. The truck passes through the ATM
  backwards — consistent with the alibi that they were together and
  intoxicated. The state possessed this footage but denied having it for
  years. [Ep. 10; Ep. 6]

11:55 A.M.
- Shane Austin, Zach Adams, and Dylan Adams are confirmed at a BP gas
  station. [Ep. 10]

NOON–~3:00 P.M.
- DirecTV installers (two employees) are at Shane Austin's trailer at
  30 Yellow Springs Road installing a satellite. TBI investigative report
  dated September 30, 2014 confirms: they specifically recalled this was
  the day of Holly Bobo's disappearance; Shane was home the entire time;
  Shane was alone and highly intoxicated, drinking beer; installation began
  around noon and took approximately three hours. [Ep. 3 Pt. 1 & 2]

MIDDAY AND EARLY AFTERNOON (at Dottie's house)
- Victor Dinsmore is working in the garage at Dottie's (he had been
  remodeling it). The cleaning lady, Debbie, arrived and they talked briefly.
- Zach Adams, Shane Austin, and Jason Autry ("A-Train") arrive in Dylan
  Adams's black Chevy truck. They ask Dinsmore for a joint; he goes outside
  and smokes it with them.
- Within two to three minutes, Zach and Shane Austin are arguing and
  fighting over "who was going to hit it first." Dinsmore does not put the
  words together at the time.
- Dinsmore tells them they cannot do that there (his job site) and they have
  to leave. Autry broke up the fight; they loaded up and left. They were
  there 10 to 15 minutes total.
- Note: Holly Bobo's personal effects are later found on the road both east
  and west of Dinsmore's property. Dinsmore does not recall selling morphine
  to any of them that day at Dottie's. [Ep. 9; Ep. 9 — DINSMORE-TRIAL]

12:17 P.M.
- Britt's ex-wife (described as a previously known accomplice on prior
  offenses) calls him to report the whole town is looking for a missing
  woman. [Ep. 4]

12:25 P.M.
- Surveillance camera near the road where Holly's phone and belongings were
  discarded captures a truck consistent with Britt's two-tone extended cab
  Ford F-150 heading southbound — consistent with the route back to Britt's
  residence. [Ep. 4]

MIDDAY AND EARLY AFTERNOON
- Deputies canvas the area for local sex offenders. Neither Terry nor Janet
  Britt's vehicle is at their residence on these canvasses. [Ep. 4]

~2:15 P.M.
- Phone records confirm a call between Jason Autry and Zach Adams at
  approximately 2:15 p.m. This is a real data point that Autry's fabricated
  trial testimony was constructed to explain. [Ep. 3 Pt. 2 — RECANT]

AFTERNOON
- Zach Adams is at Joe's Video, where his girlfriend Rebecca Earp works.
  He is high on Xanax. An argument escalates and someone calls 911. [Ep. 3
  Pt. 2]

3:27 P.M.
- 911 call timestamped 3:27 p.m. records a reported disturbance at Joe's
  Video involving Zach Adams. Audio recording is preserved. [Ep. 3 Pt. 2]

EVENING — Deputies contact Terry Britt
- Deputies pull into Britt's driveway. Before they explain why they are
  there, Britt says: "I didn't rape nobody."
- Britt insists on waiting for Janet to arrive before giving an alibi;
  they are allowed to compare stories.
- Britt's stated alibi: he and Janet were together all day, left home to
  shop for a bathtub at All Goods salvage store (he produces a receipt),
  stopped at the Newsletter so Janet could say she was taking the day off,
  home by noon.
- The All Goods receipt is fabricated — the store never sold them a bathtub.
  The Newsletter employee confirms Janet was at work that morning and was
  picked up by Terry. Phone records confirm they were apart. Britt and Janet
  later called the salvage yard to coordinate the alibi story after police
  first questioned them. [Ep. 4]

APRIL 13, 2011 — NOTE ON AUTRY TESTIMONY [TESTIMONY — FABRICATED]
- Jason Autry's 2017 trial testimony places Zach Adams, Dylan Adams, and
  Shane Austin at Shane's trailer at 30 Yellow Springs Road with Holly
  Bobo's body in the bed of a truck; Autry goes with Zach to the Tennessee
  River to dispose of the body; Holly is shot there; they return the body
  to the truck and flee; two days later Zach solicits Autry to kill Dylan.
- None of this is consistent with: cell phone data (Zach's phone never
  co-located with Holly's), Shane Austin's alibi (confirmed by DirecTV
  installers noon–3 p.m.), Zach's alibi (confirmed at Joe's Video with
  3:27 p.m. 911 call), or the ATM footage. McLendon: "It cannot be
  reconciled." [Ep. 3 Pt. 1 & 2; Ep. 1 — AUTRY-ANALYSIS]

JULY 2011
==========================================================================

JULY 5, 2011 — First TBI interview of Jason Autry
- TBI lead agent Terry Dicus and two other agents interview Autry at his
  home, approximately 90 days after Holly's abduction.
- Autry states: "I know Holly Bobo. I'm kin to her through her father."
- Autry states: "I do not think that either of them, Zach or Shayne, had
  anything to do with her kidnapping."
- Autry: "I am positive that neither of them were involved, and I know that
  I wasn't involved."
- Autry says if he knew anything useful he would turn in whoever it was for
  the reward and "buy a new truck with the money." [Ep. 3 Pt. 1; Ep. 2]

JULY 21, 2011 — Victor Dinsmore gives statement to Officer Ricky Inman
- Dinsmore has by this point moved from Decatur County to the Indianapolis
  area with his wife Sandy. He gives a statement to Officer Ricky Inman.
- He expresses frustration that Zach Adams was using him as an alibi.
- He specifically says he was not with Zach, Shane, or Jason on the day
  Holly disappeared.
- He tells Inman the fight at Dottie's happened "a few days after Holly
  disappeared" — later attributed to him having his dates mixed up because
  his wife was in the hospital the night after April 13.
- He says at this time that his wife was in the hospital the day Holly
  disappeared — this also later proves inaccurate. [Ep. 9 — DINSMORE-TRIAL]

JANUARY 2012
==========================================================================

JANUARY 2012 — Photo of Holly found in Britt's tool shed
- During the search warrant served on the Britt property (details below),
  a photograph of Holly Bobo is found in Terry Britt's tool shed. [Ep. 11]

JANUARY 2012 — Brent Booth wiretap affidavit
- TBI Special Agent Brent Booth files a 25-page affidavit with Judge Creed
  McGinley (who would later preside over the 2017 Zach Adams trial) applying
  for a wiretap on the Britt residence. Lead prosecutor Hansel McAdams signs
  off. [Ep. 4 — BRITT-AFFIDAVIT]
- Affidavit concludes: "There is probable cause to believe that Terry Britt
  and Janet Britt, his wife, working in concert, kidnapped and murdered Holly
  Bobo."
- States Britt's alibi "is fabricated by both of the target subjects."
- Notes Clint Bobo's physical description matches Britt and that "no one has
  been interviewed that more closely matches the description provided by
  Clint Bobo than Terry Britt."
- Notes Britt cut his hair after Holly disappeared, apparently to alter his
  appearance.
- Judge McGinley approves the wiretap. Jennifer Nichols later states at trial
  this was the only TBI home wiretap in Tennessee history. [Ep. 4]

JANUARY 2012 — Search warrant served at Britt property
- FBI-trained human remains detection dogs alert twice, with different
  handlers, to: a shovel, two pickaxes, and a hammer in Britt's locked shed.
  Dogs also alert on two of Britt's vehicles, including a white Vandura van.
  Tools are scattered to new locations and dogs re-identify the same items.
- Three vehicles seized. Computers seized. Britt acknowledges a hidden gun
  was on the property during the search.
- Recorded jail call, Britt to Janet regarding the cadaver dog hits: "Well,
  then somebody else put some fucking human in that motherfucker." Janet:
  "There ain't been no fucking human flesh in that old red van." (Note: she
  specifies the red van, not the Vandura the dogs actually hit on.) [Ep. 4]

JANUARY 2012 — Wiretap captures Britt
- Britt on wiretap: "I can't talk right now on the phone."
- Others ask questions about whether police "used a blacklight to search for
  blood" and "did they find the boots?" — Britt responds in ways indicating
  a guilty conscience per McLendon.
- TBI ultimately concludes the wiretap did not meet their threshold for
  action. Investigation of Britt is closed. Investigation pivots to Clint
  Bobo, then eventually to Dylan Adams and the Adams/Austin group. [Ep. 4]

SEPTEMBER 2012
==========================================================================

SEPTEMBER 12, 2012 — Second TBI interview of Jason Autry
- Autry, now in jail facing federal weapons charges (felon in possession of
  a deer rifle), gives an alibi for himself for the morning of April 13, 2011.
- Autry states: "I did not see Shane or Zach at all the day Holly was
  kidnapped. No one has ever confessed to me that they were involved in the
  kidnapping." [Ep. 3 Pt. 1; Ep. 2]

2012 — Autry, Zach Adams, and Terry Britt incarcerated at Oine Federal
Penitentiary simultaneously
- Per Autry's April 2025 jailhouse interview: Autry and Zach Adams were both
  at Oine Federal Penitentiary in 2012, both facing gun charges. This is
  the period in which Autry had extensive contact with Zach and formed the
  impressions he would later (falsely) build his testimony around. Autry
  later describes Zach as consistent in his alibi story and not someone he
  got a sense of guilt from. [AUTRY-APRIL25]
- Also at Oine in 2012: Terry Britt, also facing a gun charge. During this
  period, Britt told Autry about a .38 special that he threw in "Black
  Bottom" — a location off the side of Red Bud Lake in Lexington, Tennessee.
  Autry says this could have been the gun used to kill Holly Bobo. Britt said
  he was charged with a gun he didn't have; the .38 special was the actual
  gun, which he discarded. Britt's wife separately told Autry that Britt was
  "talking to people that weren't there" at home — seeing the ghost of a
  young girl in his kitchen who would rattle the pots and pans and vanish
  when he approached. Autry interprets this as Holly. [AUTRY-APRIL25]

SPRING 2013
==========================================================================

SPRING 2013 — Walker's jailhouse interview of Terry Britt
- Britt is in jail pending other charges (involving a different kidnapping
  and attempted rape).
- TBI lead agent Terry Dicus asks Walker to visit Britt — TBI was
  considering charging Janet Britt and wanted to know if Britt would deal.
- Walker lays out the evidence: Clint's physical description, the phone
  route north to Kelly Ridge, the route south toward Britt's property with
  belongings discarded on Goose Creek Road.
- Britt (looking at Walker seriously): "It sounds like you've got it figured
  out. I'll plead to 20 years."
- Britt adds he doesn't want to die in prison, would prefer to be housed
  with older inmates.
- Walker asks for the body's location. Britt: "I can't give you what I don't
  have."
  [Walker's later interpretation: Britt had been back to the location; the
  remains were no longer intact. Holly's skeletal remains were recovered
  scattered across a wide area in September 2014.]
- Britt offers: "If you can get me out here, I can show you something."
- Walker turns his report over to TBI. The TBI follow-up never happens.
  [Ep. 1 — WALKER]

SPRING 2014 — DENNIS BENJAMIN'S WITNESS NETWORK
==========================================================================

DENNIS BENJAMIN — BACKGROUND
- Retired Memphis police officer; known in Memphis's waterfront industry as
  "the COP" — Chief of Port. Ran things in the city's river and tugboat
  industry. Known for knowing which rules could be bent; operated in the
  gray space between law and influence.
- Spent decades in Memphis vice and gang units. Almost certainly had a long
  prior relationship with Jennifer Nichols, who prosecuted in Memphis during
  the same period.
- By 2013: retired, but still connected to law enforcement through longtime
  buddy TBI Regional Special Agent in Charge Jack Vanhuser; communicated
  with TBI agent Jeff Jackson; benefited from TBI agent Valerie Trout, who
  handled witnesses whose stories could be shaped through DCS (Department of
  Children's Services) pressure.
- Came to the case through law enforcement acquaintances — a cop friend of a
  cop friend who knew the Bobos — Benjamin sought an introduction to the
  Bobo family, offering to help. The family initially declined. A part-time
  deputy called back and pressed the introduction. Eventually Benjamin got
  in. [Ep. 11]

DENNIS BENJAMIN — WITNESS NETWORK (fully mapped in Ep. 11)
- Victor Dinsmore — early suspect turned key trial witness
- Amber Dinsmore (Victor's daughter) — was dating Carl Statler
- Carl Statler — testified at trial to incriminating statements allegedly
  made by Zach Adams
- Danielle Statler (Carl's sister) — married to Jaime Darnell
- Jaime Darnell — surfaced in TBI reports; testified against Zach at trial
- Brian Vit (Victor Dinsmore's neighbor) — off-duty cop; late-filed state
  witness; claimed to have been mowing his yard on April 13, 2011; used to
  lend credence to Autry's testimony
- Rebecca Herb (Zach Adams's girlfriend) — after breaking up with Zach,
  began dating Jason Hugley
- Jason Hugley — investigators later said Zach made incriminating statements
  to him
- Tara Carrington (Jason Hugley's ex-girlfriend) — mother of children in DCS
  custody; her DCS caseworker was Clint Bobo (Holly's brother); Tara's
  statements led to Zach Adams's first arrest on unrelated charges that were
  eventually dropped
[Ep. 11]

SPRING 2014 — Dennis Benjamin tracks down Victor Dinsmore in Indianapolis
- Dinsmore had relocated to the Indianapolis area with his wife Sandy after
  Holly's disappearance.
- Benjamin presents himself as a retired cop working for the Bobo family,
  alongside TBI and FBI. Finds Dinsmore and records at least one confrontation.
- In the recorded confrontation, Benjamin threatens Dinsmore with the death
  penalty and leverages his TBI connections: "I can get you on the phone
  right now with Jack Van Hoosier with TBI."
- Benjamin tells Dinsmore "Autry's wanting a deal and Zach's wanting a deal
  for the body" — using other witnesses' status as leverage.
- Dinsmore flatly and repeatedly denies seeing Holly in any truck or at
  Dottie's: "I did not see that fucking girl. I did not know her before or
  after. I had no fucking bid in her abduction."
- Dinsmore refuses to cooperate; meeting ends badly. [Ep. 9; Ep. 11 —
  DINSMORE-COACHING]

MARCH 5, 2014 — Dinsmore gives statement to TBI agent Valerie Trout
- This is after Zach's February 28, 2014 arrest (see below). The arrest had
  been on the news; Dinsmore knew investigators had searched Zach's house
  and grandfather's house.
- Dinsmore says Zach had hidden his grandfather's white Nissan truck, but
  does not specifically say it was hidden at Dinsmore's property. He also
  states his wife was in the hospital the day Holly disappeared (this later
  proves inaccurate as well). [Ep. 9 — DINSMORE-TRIAL]

FEBRUARY 28, 2014 — Zach Adams arrested; TBI press conference
- TBI and FBI execute search warrants; Zach Adams arrested on charges related
  to Holly's murder.
- TBI Director Mark Gwyn and DA Hansel McAdams hold a press conference.
  Gwyn: "Hopefully we'll be able to return Holly home safely to her parents.
  That's our goal."
- Note: At the time of arrest, the state falsely told the grand jury there
  was a video of a rape that occurred — this claim is proven false. Two other
  men were later indicted for allegedly having and then destroying this
  never-existing video. [Ep. 11]

==========================================================================
PRE-SEPTEMBER 2014 — DYLAN ADAMS AND DENNIS BENJAMIN
==========================================================================

DYLAN ADAMS — BACKGROUND
- Zach's younger brother; documented learning disabilities; received a
  special education completion certificate (not a standard high school
  diploma). Highly suggestible and vulnerable to manipulation.
- Dr. Spirko's neuropsychological analysis: Dylan's deficits cluster in
  frontal lobe tasks — specifically source memory (distinguishing firsthand
  from suggested memory) and distinguishing false from true memory. This
  makes him an ideal suggestibility victim for a skilled interrogator.
  [Ep. 8; Ep. 11]

DYLAN ADAMS — FEDERAL CASE
- Dylan Adams faces federal gun charges (selling a firearm; multiple
  felonies).
- His federal docket has approximately 70 entries. Approximately 25 documents
  are missing from the public record entirely. The last 6–8 entries are
  sealed. The terms of his plea deal remain unknown outside a small group.
- Beth Boswell plays a dual role: she is simultaneously Dylan's
  court-appointed defense attorney on the federal case; a federal and state
  prosecutor handling multiple Holly Bobo-related cases (the Terry Britt
  plea, the Jason Autry plea, and others); and in a romantic relationship
  with Dennis Benjamin, who is embedded in the investigation as a witness
  handler.
- McLendon: "There is just about zero chance that Dennis Benjamin has not
  got a long relationship with Jennifer Nichols." [Ep. 8; Ep. 11]

AUGUST 2014 — Dylan Adams's plea deal and placement with Benjamin
- Dylan settles his federal charges in August 2014.
- The terms include a required condition: Dylan must go live at Dennis
  Benjamin's Memphis home with Benjamin and his wife as a condition of his
  federal probation. Dylan had never met Benjamin until the day before his
  plea bargain and sentence.
- Benjamin has full unsupervised access to Dylan at his home for
  approximately 6 weeks. Benjamin takes Dylan to a relative's children's
  birthday party; Dylan is left unattended in the house with Benjamin's wife
  and daughter. [Ep. 8; Ep. 9; Ep. 11]

DURING DYLAN'S ~6 WEEKS AT BENJAMIN'S HOME (August–September 2014)
- Benjamin coaches Dylan repeatedly on the story he is to tell TBI. The
  details he plants track directly to what Jeff Jackson and Brent Booth feed
  Dylan during the September 17, 2014 interrogation.
- Benjamin communicates with TBI agents Jeff Jackson and Brent Booth
  throughout this period.
- McLendon: "Dylan didn't confess to a detective. Dennis Benjamin confessed
  for him." [Ep. 8; Ep. 11]

SEPTEMBER 2014
==========================================================================

SEPTEMBER 1, 2014 — Holly Bobo's remains found
- Holly's skeletal remains recovered approximately 20 miles from her home at
  Kelly Ridge, in a remote wooded area near (but not visible from)
  Interstate 40. This is the exact location where her cell phone was
  stationary for 25–30 minutes on the morning of April 13, 2011.
- Walker had searched this broad area himself but it is very large. He was
  not present at the recovery. [Ep. 1]

DAYS AFTER REMAINS FOUND (early-to-mid September 2014)
- Benjamin contacts authorities to report a "confession" Dylan allegedly made
  to him the night before. This is approximately 10 days after Holly's
  remains were found.
- Benjamin then brings Dylan to the TBI; Dylan tries to repeat this supposed
  confession in the presence of TBI agents Jeff Jackson and Brent Booth.
  [Ep. 8; Ep. 9; Ep. 11]

HOW THE INTERROGATION PROCEEDS:
- Agents use a consistent pattern: introduce a fact Dylan has not stated;
  express it as confirmation of something Dylan said; then ask a question
  whose answer treats the injected fact as established.
- When Dylan pushes back, the agents simply repeat the false framing with
  more confidence until Dylan acquiesces.
- Dylan cannot recall things one would expect him to know — phone numbers
  for people he called regularly, for example — consistent with his
  documented memory deficits.
- Dylan describes Holly as wearing "long pants, pink shirt" — the description
  everyone publicly knew at the time. He mentions no blood, no specific
  injuries consistent with the crime scene.
- Dylan blushes visibly when agents introduce sexual terms; Dr. Spirko: "A
  person who actually participated in a violent rape would not use polite,
  clinical, embarrassed language when describing it."
- When asked about blood at the scene, Dylan describes minor injuries (black
  eye, small cut on lip). Agents note two pools of blood had formed on the
  floor — if Dylan's account were true, his DNA would be in that blood.
  No DNA evidence ever connects Dylan to any crime scene.
- Agents tell Dylan what he is supposed to remember rather than asking him
  to recall independently.
- The agents appear frustrated that Benjamin has not adequately prepped
  Dylan; they must supply details repeatedly that Dylan cannot produce.
  [Ep. 8; full interrogation transcript]

APRIL 25, 2025 — Jason Autry full jailhouse interview (recant, extended)

  mother, confirmed by Brent Booth. This is how Autry knew the .38 caliber

  the woods was a logger report in the case files — loggers in the area
  that morning who thought they saw three men together and that one appeared
  to have been struggling. Autry does not know if the logger report was
  accurate; he borrowed the placement from it.
- Fence/gate error confirmed: Autry testified about a fence and gate at
  Shane's trailer that did not exist on April 13, 2011. It went up a few
  months later when Shane got a puppy. "You did a pretty good job. I mean,
  but I don't know that."
- Gun testimony error: Autry testified about a gun connected to Shane — a
  .357 with a spark (muzzle brake) on the end, long barrel, black; Shane's
  father Jimmy had shown Autry this gun. Autry told Victor Dinsmore this
  gun belonged to Shane; Dinsmore then produced or identified it for police.
  But the gun actually found does not match Shane's .357 — the found gun
  was a .32 or .38 caliber. These are different guns. Jimmy Austin still
  has the .357, Autry believes.
- ATM video confirmation: The ATM security guard at the bank where Shane
  used the ATM offered TBI video footage corroborating the exact time and
  date of the transaction. Per what the interviewer tells Autry: TBI
  declined to take the footage.
- Zach's consistent alibi: Autry confirms Zach had five to seven consistent
  TBI reports, all with the same alibi — getting Xanax bars with Shane and
  Dylan, confirmed by bank statements. Zach also went to Joe's Video where
  his girlfriend Rebecca Herp was working, was intoxicated on Xanax, got in
  an altercation, and someone called 911 — but no one responded because law
