I have been getting lots of questions about the first series of the Trial & Error podcast. I was forced by the state of Tennessee to remove it from all platforms, and I am not allowed to discuss anything about the case it covered. The prosecutors and law enforcement agencies allege I have violated their confidentiality in exposing material from the investigative files that they insist should remain hidden from public view. The pro tem prosecutor, Amy Weirich, is presently pursuing multiple criminal contempt charges against me, each of which carries 10 days in jail, up to a maximum of 6 months. I do not yet know how many total counts I am facing. Amy said in court there were too many for her to estimate. One count is for appearing as a guest on season 9 of Undisclosed - Toward Justice, an outstanding podcast hosted by University of South Carolina Law Professor Colin Miller and attorney/author Rabia Chaudry. It focuses on wrongful convictions and boasts prior highly acclaimed seasons already linked to 15 exonerations. Another count is for responding to a Facebook question about whether a suspect I shall not name passed multiple polygraphs. Weirich will be following up in writing with a comprehensive list of the other counts so I have an opportunity to prepare my defense ahead of my February 18, 2027, hearing in Carroll County. 

My apologies to anyone who has messaged me and not received a response, as well as to all the tax payers throughout Tennessee who are funding this aggressive ongoing prosecution of me - it’s cost me tens of thousands of dollars and more in lost income than I care to estimate just to defend it, so I can’t imagine what it’s cost all of you to fund it. Most of all, my heart goes out to the many victims of actual crimes in Amy Weirich’s district whose cases are not receiving the same degree of attention and resource allocation because of her preoccupation with silencing me.
